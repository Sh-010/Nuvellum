# Post-launch editorial product backlog

This file captures Nuvellum improvements that should **not** distract from the current launch/stabilisation work. Revisit only after the website, newsroom, Brief and social distribution can operate reliably on their own.

## Editorial identity

Nuvellum should not become only a polished automated news summariser. The long-term structure should distinguish between:

- **News / Briefs** — fast factual reporting and concise updates.
- **Analysis** — context, implications, causes and what happens next.
- **Essays & Ideas** — authored long-form work with a recognisable voice.
- **Explainers** — timelines, maps, key actors, chronology and background.
- **The Nuvellum Brief** — the daily curated email product.

The editorial aim behind “Beyond the headline” is that readers can tell the difference between an event Nuvellum reports and an argument, interpretation or piece of original work that Nuvellum authors.

## Authorial voice

Add real author pages when there is enough original work to justify them:

- portrait/avatar;
- short biography;
- areas of focus;
- authored reporting, analysis and essays;
- optional social links;
- visible bylines on original work.

## Audio

Start with a restrained **Listen to this article** player using the TTS/audio infrastructure already built for Shorts.

Later, consider a distinct **Nuvellum Brief — Audio Edition** rather than a separate podcast stack.

## Interactive editorial formats

Use interactive elements selectively where they genuinely explain a story:

- timelines for wars, elections, court cases and investigations;
- maps for geopolitical stories;
- small charts for economic and business reporting;
- “key people / institutions” cards;
- chronology/background modules.

The existing World Explorer should remain the flagship interactive geography product rather than adding gratuitous interactives everywhere.

## Personalised Brief

Do not change the stable Brief system during launch.

Later, subscribers may be allowed to select sections/topics such as:

- World
- Business
- Tech
- Culture
- Screen & Play
- Sports

Possible future preferences: daily/weekly frequency and topic emphasis.

## Community

Comments/forum/community work is intentionally late-stage.

Do not add accounts, comments or forums until Nuvellum has a meaningful recurring readership and a moderation model. Community features bring spam, abuse, account security, notification and legal overhead.

## Signature Nuvellum products

Prioritise distinct editorial products over feature accumulation:

- **Nuvellum Context** — a designed background/context box in major stories.
- **The Situation** — one major event explained visually.
- **Five Things That Matter** — daily or weekly editorial selection.
- **Nuvellum Essays** — serious long-form work.
- **The Archive** — an editorial library rather than a plain date index.
- **Country dossiers** connected to World Explorer: recent stories, key facts, chronology, leadership and relationships.
- **Distinctive criticism** in film, games and anime rather than generic entertainment summaries.

## Order of work

1. Finish launch/stabilisation.
2. Let newsroom + Brief + social distribution operate reliably.
3. Build original editorial output and authorship.
4. Add audio and selective interactives.
5. Add personalised Brief preferences.
6. Consider community only after an audience exists.

This backlog is intentionally deferred. Do not reopen these items merely because launch work feels finished for a few hours; first establish several days of stable autonomous operation.
