## Shooting on Manhattan subway platform

New York City police are searching for a suspect following a fatal shooting on a subway platform in Manhattan on Saturday afternoon. According to the New York Police Department, the incident occurred at approximately 12:21 p.m. on the northbound E train platform of the 53rd Street–7th Avenue subway station.

The suspect displayed a firearm and struck a man in his 30s once in the chest. Emergency medical services responded to the scene and transported the victim to NYC Health + Hospitals/Bellevue, where he was pronounced dead. The identity of the victim has not been released.

## Police search and suspect description

The suspect is wanted for homicide within the Midtown North Precinct/Transit District 1. Following the shooting, the suspect fled on foot heading northbound on 7th Avenue.

The NYPD has released surveillance photographs showing the suspect wearing a red tracksuit, a black vest, and a black face mask, while carrying a backpack.

Authorities are asking anyone with information to contact the Crime Stoppers tip line at 1-800-577-TIPS, submit tips online, or reach out via X at @NYPDTips. The investigation remains active.
