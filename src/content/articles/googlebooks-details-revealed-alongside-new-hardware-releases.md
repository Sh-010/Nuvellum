## Googlebooks laptops and updates

Google has revealed a significant amount of detail concerning its Googlebooks laptops. The devices are positioned as strong options for users seeking a laptop to pair with an Android phone. 

According to the company's announcements, Googlebooks will receive 10 years of software updates, a commitment intended to demonstrate long-term support for the platform. The initial wave of devices will feature hardware from five manufacturers: Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, and Lenovo.

## Additional hardware drops

The Googlebooks announcement accompanies several other hardware developments in the tech sector. Meta has introduced new smart glasses under the Ray-Ban Meta Audio line, which omit cameras while retaining microphones and speakers for music playback and AI interactions, alongside offering longer battery life and new styles.

Microsoft has also updated its peripheral lineup with the second-generation Surface Mouse, priced at $79.99. This updated model includes haptic feedback, a dedicated action button, and the capability to pair with up to three distinct devices.
