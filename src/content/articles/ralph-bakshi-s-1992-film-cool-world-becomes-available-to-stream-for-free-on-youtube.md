## Premise and production history

The film follows Jack Deebs (Gabriel Byrne), an ex-convict who spent a 10-year prison sentence for murder. During his time in prison, Jack created a comic book titled *Cool World*, centered on a femme fatale named Holli Would (Kim Basinger), which he based on recurring visions. Upon his release, Jack is physically transported into Cool World, a dimension inhabited by cartoons known as "doodles." Holli aims to use Jack to transform into a human so she can escape into the real world, a plan that threatens to destabilize both dimensions and draws opposition from Frank Harris (Brad Pitt), a human police officer residing in Cool World.

According to comments Bakshi made to IGN in 2004, *Cool World* originally began as a horror story about the child of a cartoonist and a cartoon traveling to the real world to kill his father. After Paramount purchased the project, the studio hired Bakshi to direct but provided a different script on the first day of production, shifting the story into a PG-13 action fantasy.
