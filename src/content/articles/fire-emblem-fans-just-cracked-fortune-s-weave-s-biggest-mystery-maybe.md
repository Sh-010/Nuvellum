Players of *Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave* have been piecing together clues from across the game's routes to determine the true identity of its silent protagonist, Eshmel. While the game never directly states who Eshmel was before being created as a Mediator by Fortuna and the Kunlun village leader, a detailed theory shared across community platforms like Reddit and GameFAQs suggests a surprising connection.

## The origins of the Mediator

At the start of *Fortune's Weave*, Fortuna plucks a soul from the aether to inhabit the newly created body of Eshmel, describing it as a way to make amends and restore light to Dagda. Throughout the game, Eshmel acts as the Herald of the Pale Raven, leading Dagda against Balor and the underworld without any explicit confirmation of their past life.

However, players exploring specific optional content—such as Anna's paralogue and Cai's route—have noted several striking parallels between Eshmel and Solel, a character belonging to the divine Trident class.

## Evidence from weapons and lore

Among the strongest pieces of evidence cited by fans is Eshmel's spear. Once the bandages are removed and the weapon is restored in Part 3, characters note its familiar appearance, matching Solel's spear. Additionally, Solel's battle stance closely mirrors Eshmel's.

Lore connections further reinforce the theory. Game dialogue establishes that Nabatean souls do not rejoin the standard cycle of death and rebirth, instead being drawn back to Sothis, leaving precedent within the universe for souls to be placed in unexpected vessels. Furthermore, the narrative arcs of the Flame Lords highlight Solel's past failures regarding his family, nation, and ethical duties, aligning with Fortuna's stated purpose for Eshmel's soul.

While Nintendo has not officially confirmed the connection, the theory offers a cohesive explanation for the game's lingering background mysteries and character parallels.
