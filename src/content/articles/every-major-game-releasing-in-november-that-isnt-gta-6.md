While the anticipated launch of Rockstar's *Grand Theft Auto 6* in November 2026 has led many developers to shift their schedules to avoid the release, several games are still scheduled to arrive during the month for players seeking alternatives.

## Early November releases

* **Gravhounds** (Nov. 2 - Windows PC, Xbox Series X): An early access co-op shooter featuring gun-toting dogs on an alien planet with base building and gravity-bending physics.
* **Godzilla: Destroy All Monsters Melee Remastered** (Nov. 3 - Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox Series X): A remastered version of the cult classic kaiju brawler featuring online multiplayer 24 years after the original release.
* **World of Warcraft: Forever** (Nov. 4 - Windows PC): An all-new version of Blizzard's MMO featuring retro graphics and brand-new content inspired by its 2000s heyday.
* **The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time** (Nov. 5 - Nintendo Switch 2): A modern remake of the title featuring updated visuals, modernized controls, and a jump button.
* **Escape Academy 2: Back 2 School** (Nov. 5 - PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox Series X): A multiplayer puzzle game featuring escape rooms and local split-screen cooperative play.

## Mid-November releases

* **Warhammer Survivors** (Nov. 10 - Android, iOS, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox Series X): A Warhammer-themed entry in the *Vampire Survivors* genre style, developed by the team behind *Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun*.
* **Barbie Rewind** (Nov. 12 - Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, Xbox Series X): A retro collection compiling Barbie games released between 1991 and 2007.
* **Sonic Pico Park** (Nov. 12 - Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, Windows PC): A Sonic the Hedgehog-themed 2D puzzle platformer spin-off of the multiplayer *Pico Park* series, released as part of the character's 35th anniversary.
* **Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy** (Nov. 12 - Nintendo Switch 2): An updated version of the strategy game bundled with a stage-structured challenge mode called Dandori Academy.
* **Milki Delivery** (Nov. 17 - Windows PC): A cozy 2D indie game putting players in charge of a dairy delivery service using a bicycle to deliver milk from cows to local residents.
