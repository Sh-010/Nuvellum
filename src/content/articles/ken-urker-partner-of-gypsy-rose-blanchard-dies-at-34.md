## Background and reality television appearance

Urker was featured alongside Blanchard on the Lifetime reality television series *Gypsy Rose: Life After Lockup*, which aired for two seasons.

Blanchard and Urker originally connected through a penpal program while Blanchard was serving a prison sentence for her involvement in the murder of her mother, Dee Dee Blanchard. After an initial engagement ended, Blanchard married and subsequently divorced Ryan Anderson before reconciling with Urker in 2024. Urker and Blanchard welcomed a daughter, Aurora, in December 2024.
