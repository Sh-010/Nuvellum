The developers behind *Battlefield 6* have addressed player feedback concerning controversial cosmetic items released during Season 5. Battlefield Studios shared background information on its official Discord server regarding two specific skins that drew attention from the community.

## Developer response to cosmetic issues

The items in question included a character skin featuring a misspelling of the word "security" on an armband and another outfit, designated as the Midnight Mirage skin, equipped with five pistol holsters. The items sparked discussions and memes across social media platforms following their release on October 13.

Addressing the typo on the McNeil skin, developers acknowledged that the mistake fell short of expected quality standards. The studio stated that it is examining internal workflows to understand how the error occurred and to refine quality control procedures. A fix for the misspelling is scheduled to arrive when *Battlefield 6* Season 5 updates in November.

## Intentional design choices

While the typo is slated for correction, Battlefield Studios confirmed that the Midnight Mirage skin with multiple pistol holsters was an intentional creative decision. The developer described the design as a nod to action movie tropes in which operatives carry backup weapons strapped across their gear, though it acknowledged that releasing it alongside the typo skin contributed to unintended visual confusion.

The communication from the development team and Electronic Arts did not comment on separate generative AI accusations raised by some players in the aftermath of the cosmetic launches. *Battlefield 6* Season 5 continues to roll out additional content in waves, including new maps, events, and tools spanning upcoming updates in November and December.
