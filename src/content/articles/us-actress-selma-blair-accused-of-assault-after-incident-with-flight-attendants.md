## Incident on Delta Airlines Flight 938

US actress Selma Blair was arrested and transported to a hospital following an incident on a Delta Airlines flight travelling from Los Angeles to New York. Airport officials reported responding to a call on Sunday concerning a physically abusive passenger aboard Delta Airlines Flight 938, which occurred around 2300 local time.

According to the New York Port Authority, two flight attendants were injured during the incident, and one was transported to a hospital. Air traffic control audio obtained by CBS News indicated that the flight crew allegedly restrained Blair during the flight, and the pilot requested medical personnel for a "bloody nose as well as some bite marks."

## FBI Investigation and Medical Response

The FBI, which holds jurisdiction over investigations into assaults aboard aircraft, confirmed it is investigating a potential assault on the flight but declined to provide further comments. It remains unclear whether Blair will face charges, and the BBC has contacted New York prosecutors.

Blair's publicist stated that the actress experienced a medical emergency leading to the alleged conduct. "Upon landing, she received immediate medical attention and is currently in stable condition," her publicist said.

A spokesperson for Delta Airlines stated that law enforcement responded upon the flight's arrival at John F Kennedy International Airport and noted there was no operational impact. "Delta has zero tolerance for unruly or unlawful behavior, as the safety of our customers and crew always comes first," the airline said.

## Background

Blair, 54, is known for her roles in films including *Legally Blonde*, *Hellboy*, and *Cruel Intentions*. She previously stepped back from film work following a 2018 multiple sclerosis diagnosis, though she announced in 2021 that she was in remission. In 2016, she was involved in another flight incident resulting from a mixture of medication and alcohol, for which she subsequently issued a public apology.
