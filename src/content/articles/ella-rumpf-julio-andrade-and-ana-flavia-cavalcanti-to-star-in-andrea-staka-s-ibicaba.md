## Cast and production details

Actors Ella Rumpf, Júlio Andrade and Ana Flávia Cavalcanti have been cast in Andrea Štaka’s upcoming historical drama, “Ibicaba: O Paraíso Prometido.” 

The film will be produced by Brazil's MyMama Entertainment banner in a co-production with Switzerland’s Cinédokké Sagl and Brazil’s MMM Carioca. Production is scheduled to begin next year, with filming locations set in Switzerland and Cordeirópolis, a municipality located in the central region of the state of São Paulo.

## Plot and historical basis

The movie is adapted from Eveline Hasler’s novel "Ibicaba: O Paraíso nas Cabeças" and draws upon real-life events at the titular farm in rural São Paulo.

Set in 1855, the narrative follows a Swiss working-class single mother who travels to Brazil following the prospect of a better life. Upon arrival, she encounters a system characterized by debt, surveillance and exploitation within a society structured around slavery. Her journey intersects with Inocência, an enslaved Black woman with knowledge of healing and the land who creates her own forms of resistance.

To aid the project's historical accuracy, the production collaborated with Black historian Caróu Oliveira, who studies 19th-century slavery and the daily lives of Black women.

## Director's background

Director Andrea Štaka first encountered Hasler’s novel during her adolescence. In preparation for the film, Štaka has been studying Portuguese to communicate directly with cast and crew members and closely engage with the characters' dialogues.

Štaka previously won the Golden Leopard at the Locarno Film Festival and the best film prize at the Sarajevo Film Festival in 2006 for "Das Fräulein." "Ibicaba" previously secured the co-production development award at the Berlinale Co-Production Market.
