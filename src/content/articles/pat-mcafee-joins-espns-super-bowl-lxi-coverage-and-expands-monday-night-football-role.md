## Expanding to the NFL

Pat McAfee is bringing his *Field Pass* show from college football to the NFL. During next year's Super Bowl LXI, the talk show talent will host a version of *Field Pass with the Pat McAfee Show* as one of three alternate telecasts for the game.

*Field Pass* is scheduled to air on ESPNU. The other alternate telecasts for Super Bowl LXI include *Super Bowl LXI Playbook with Next Gen Stats* on NFL Network and the *Manningcast* on ESPN2.

## Monday Night Football schedule

In addition to the Super Bowl coverage, *Field Pass with the Pat McAfee Show* will serve as an alternate telecast for a series of Monday Night Football games this season.

The run is set to debut in Week 6 for the matchup between the Washington Commanders and the San Francisco 49ers. It marks the first of five Monday Night Football editions for the program, concluding during Week 18 on January 9.

McAfee will continue operating from a field-level position alongside Dan Orlovsky and Darius Butler. They will be joined by Connor Campbell, Ty Schmit, and Tone Digs broadcasting from an in-stadium suite. Separately, the *MNF Playbook with Next Gen Stats* broadcast on NFL Network begins a week earlier in Week 5 for the Buffalo Bills and Los Angeles Rams game, marking the first of seven Monday editions for that program.
