## Transition to new role

Efrain Miron has secured a newly established position as chief commercial officer for the product and technology team at Skydance. Miron transitions to this capacity following his departure from his previous post as executive vice president and head of content strategy and licensing for Paramount direct-to-consumer. His appointment was communicated internally by Dane Glasgow, Skydance's chief product officer, to whom Miron will report.

Glasgow highlighted Miron's past contributions in an internal memo, noting that he had been instrumental in expanding streaming operations across platforms like Paramount+ and Pluto TV since joining Paramount after the finalization of the Skydance merger in August 2025. Glasgow credited Miron with utilizing live sports to expand streaming and coordinating the distribution of original content globally.

## Background and restructuring context

Miron's movement follows corporate changes implemented by JB Perrette, co-chair and chief business officer of Skydance's television and direct-to-consumer divisions. Miron had originally joined Paramount's direct-to-consumer division alongside Jane Wiseman after working with former unit head Cindy Holland at Netflix and Sister.

## Future commercial strategy

In his new capacity, Miron will guide the commercial strategy for initiatives extending past core streaming services. According to Glasgow's internal communication, these efforts will involve exploring emerging fan ecosystems and entertainment domains, with Miron establishing business models and partnerships to support these ventures in collaboration with various company business units.
