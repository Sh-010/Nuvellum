## Police respond to active shooter in Bend

Two people have been hospitalised and a shooter was found dead following a shooting on Thursday at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon. Law enforcement responded to reports of an active shooter at the primary school, which serves students in kindergarten through fifth grade and is located more than 100 miles south-east of Portland.

Sheila Miller, a spokesperson for the Bend Police Department, stated during a press conference that officers were alerted to the scene at 1:00 pm local time and arrived approximately two minutes later. According to Miller, officers received radio confirmation that the suspect was down seconds after their arrival. Authorities have not yet confirmed who shot the suspect, noting it may have been a bystander.

## School lockdown and student reunification

The Bend-La Pine School District placed Silver Rail Elementary School under lockdown as the situation unfolded. In subsequent social media updates, the district stated that law enforcement escorted students in buses to a separate location where families could reunite with their children.

Medics transported at least two people to a local hospital, according to police, who stated that the status of the victims was not yet known.
