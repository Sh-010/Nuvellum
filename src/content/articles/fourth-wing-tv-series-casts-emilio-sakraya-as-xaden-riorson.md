## Casting announcement for Prime Video adaptation

Actor Emilio Sakraya has been cast as Xaden Riorson in the upcoming television adaptation of Rebecca Yarros’s bestselling romantasy book series *Fourth Wing*. Sakraya will star opposite Esther McGregor, who portrays Violet Sorrengail in the Amazon Prime Video series. Amazon declined to comment on the casting news.

Sakraya’s previous acting credits include roles in German and U.S. film and television productions such as *Rhinegold*, *Tribes of Europa*, *Warrior Nun*, and *Those About to Die*. He is also scheduled to appear in the third season of *One Piece*.

## Background on the series and production

*Fourth Wing* is set at the Basgiath War College, where candidates compete to become elite dragon riders in the military of Navarre. The story follows twenty-year-old Violet Sorrengail, who is ordered by her military general mother to enter the college.

Yarros’s *The Empyrean* book series began with the release of *Fourth Wing* in May 2023, followed by sequels *Iron Flame* and *Onyx Storm*, as well as a 2025 novella titled *Threshing Day*. 

The television adaptation was ordered to series by Amazon in May. It is produced by Michael B. Jordan’s Outlier Society and Amazon MGM Studios. Meredith Averill serves as showrunner and executive producer alongside Yarros, Jordan, Outlier president Elizabeth Raposo, Jonathan Nolan, and Athena Wickham. Lisa Joy is an executive producer and is set to direct the first episode. Additional executive producers include Liz Pelletier and Sherryl Clark for Premeditated Productions, while Stefano Agosto serves as co-executive producer.
