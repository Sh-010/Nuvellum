## Instagram account disabled

Meta removed the Instagram account of British comedian Russell Brand following his recent criminal conviction in London, according to screenshots shared by Brand on X. The profile page stated that the account was disabled because his activity violated community standards. A company spokesperson confirmed to media outlets that the page was taken down due to policy violations.

Brand announced the removal on X, expressing gratitude to the platform for maintaining his active profile there, which retains millions of followers.

## Prison sentence and ongoing investigations

A judge sentenced Brand to ten and a half years in prison following his conviction on one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault. During the sentencing, the judge described the comedian as a sexual predator who committed criminal offenses rather than mere outrageous behavior.

London law enforcement authorities confirmed that investigators are reviewing additional allegations of rape reported by two other women. Prior to his criminal trial, Brand had faced multiple accusations of sexual misconduct, leading to previous monetization restrictions on his video channel.
