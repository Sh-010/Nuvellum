Kenan “Ken” Urker, the partner of Gypsy Rose Blanchard, was found dead inside his home in Louisiana on Thursday on his 34th birthday. Authorities described the incident as an apparent drug overdose.

## Welfare check and police findings

The Lafourche Parish Sheriff’s Office confirmed that Urker, who was the father of Blanchard’s daughter, was discovered after relatives requested a police welfare check. According to the sheriff’s office report, Anita Blanchard and her husband—Gypsy Rose Blanchard’s half-brother—went to Urker’s residence and saw him unconscious through an outside window. A man accompanying them entered through a window and found Urker unresponsive, cold to the touch, and not breathing.

Deputies arrived to find Urker lying on his back in bed. Investigators located drug paraphernalia, including syringes and a spoon next to a brown powdery substance, in an adjacent bathroom. A syringe and a handgun were also found on the nightstand. Captain Brennan Matherne stated that the death remains under investigation, though investigators had not found any signs of foul play.

## Statements from Gypsy Rose Blanchard

In an interview published on Friday, Gypsy Rose Blanchard stated that she believed Urker died from an overdose, though she was uncertain if it was deliberate. She noted that Urker “was not in a good state of mind lately.” 

In a separate statement posted to social media, Blanchard addressed public scrutiny surrounding Urker. She wrote that he had endured extensive cyberbullying and public scrutiny across platforms including TikTok and Reddit, expressing her belief that the harassment had a profound impact on him.

Urker and Blanchard first connected while Blanchard was serving a prison sentence for her role in the 2015 killing of her mother, Clauddine “Dee Dee” Blanchard. After periods apart and separate marriages, the couple rekindled their relationship in April 2024 and welcomed a daughter later that year.
