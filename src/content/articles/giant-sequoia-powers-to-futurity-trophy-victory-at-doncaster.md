## Futurity Trophy success at Doncaster

Giant Sequoia delivered a strong performance to claim the William Hill Futurity Trophy at Doncaster, securing a record-extending 13th victory in the race for trainer Aidan O'Brien and practically ensuring him the trainers' championship. 

Stablemate Sergei Diaghilev set the early pace before the contest developed into a more tactical affair approaching the final two furlongs. Ryan Moore and the favourite, Noble Exhibit, made their move with a furlong and a half remaining, but Giant Sequoia raced strongly alongside them. Ridden by Dylan Browne McMonagle, the 11-1 shot quickened decisively to draw three lengths clear of Gran Dia, who finished second ahead of Noble Exhibit.

O'Brien noted that the colt had underperformed in his previous outing at the National Stakes due to the stable's horses being below par at the time. Describing the winner as classy, the trainer indicated that the Frankel colt could target major Classic races next season, including the Guineas and the Derby, over distances ranging from a mile to a mile and a half.

## Milestone for Browne McMonagle

The victory marked Browne McMonagle's first Group One win for the Ballydoyle stable. The jockey described the mount as a developing young horse who executed the race plan smoothly.

"He's a horse who has developed plenty throughout the year, he's a big boy to sit on," Browne McMonagle said after the race.

## Bengal Tiger dominates Prospect Stakes

Earlier at the Doncaster meeting, O'Brien also found success with Bengal Tiger in the William Hill Remembering Sue Wright Prospect Stakes. Ridden by Ryan Moore, the 10-11 favourite tracked the early leader before pulling clear inside the final furlong to score by three and a half lengths over Hi Corbett Court.

O'Brien suggested that the winner would easily stay further than the six-furlong trip and also viewed him as a potential Classic contender following a strong finish in testing ground.
