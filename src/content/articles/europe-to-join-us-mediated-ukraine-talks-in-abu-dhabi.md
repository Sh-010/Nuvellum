## French Confirmation of Participation

European nations will have a presence at the upcoming discussions aimed at concluding the conflict in Ukraine, according to French Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot. Speaking on Sunday, Barrot stated that France would attend the sessions scheduled for Tuesday and Wednesday in Abu Dhabi, ensuring that the perspective of Europe remains present during the diplomatic process.

The meetings are organized under American mediation. Previously, the scheduled negotiations were structured as a trilateral format involving officials from Ukraine, Russia, and the United States, without public confirmation of the specific delegates.

## Differing Approaches to European Representation

While Ukrainian leadership advocated for the inclusion of European allies, Moscow had previously indicated that European representatives would not have a place at the negotiating table. Barrot noted that while past discussions occurred with varying levels of European involvement, continental representatives would definitely take part this time.

Ahead of the Abu Dhabi summit, American, European, Ukrainian, and NATO officials gathered in Brussels on Thursday. Jared Kushner, serving as the U.S. president's envoy, participated via videolink and mentioned that Washington was pursuing a broader European security framework to help establish enduring peace conditions.

## Wider Security Architecture Concerns

European allies have approached the American strategy with caution due to historical Russian demands regarding regional security. Prior to the 2022 invasion, Moscow issued an ultimatum seeking the withdrawal of NATO troops from eastern Europe, a demand that the alliance rejected.

Kushner stated that Washington intends to consult closely with European partners on the sidelines of the Abu Dhabi meetings and transition to a broader multilateral format with direct participation if headway is achieved.
