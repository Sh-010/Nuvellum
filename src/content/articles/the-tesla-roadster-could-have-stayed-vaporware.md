Nearly a decade after Elon Musk first announced plans to build a next-generation sports car, the Tesla Roadster is expected to make its production-ready debut at an event in Waco, Texas. The vehicle arrives as automakers scale back electric vehicle plans amid an EV market facing high energy prices, political turmoil, and consumer uncertainty.

## A decade of delays and high promises

First revealed in 2017, the new Roadster became defined by its elusiveness and outrageous promises. Musk previously stated that the vehicle can accelerate from 0 to 60 miles per hour in under two seconds, reach a top speed exceeding 250 miles per hour, and feature an optional "SpaceX package" with cold-gas thrusters. 

Due to years of missed deadlines, several reservation holders—including OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and YouTuber Marques Brownlee—canceled their orders under the assumption that the project would fail. Tesla itself has already achieved some of the claimed performance goals through other models, such as the Model S Plaid released in 2021 with a 1.99-second 0–60 time.

## Shifting priorities at Tesla

As the Roadster finally heads to production, Tesla's corporate focus has largely shifted toward autonomous technology and robotics. Musk has stated that the Roadster will be the final Tesla vehicle built for human driving, with future efforts centering on autonomy. Tesla recently ended production of its original flagship models, the Model S and Model X, to clear space for manufacturing humanoid Optimus robots.

Furthermore, the electric hypercar market has proved challenging for manufacturers. Companies such as Rimac, Pininfarina, and Lotus faced low sales volumes, severe production delays, and weak demand for their high-end electric models. Executives like Bugatti CEO Mate Rimac have noted that wealthy buyers of exotic cars overwhelmingly prefer internal combustion engines for their mechanical characteristics and emotional prestige.

Whether the Roadster achieves massive sales volume or remains a limited-run ultra-high-end supercar, its arrival marks a grounded artifact of a future Tesla has already begun to move past.
