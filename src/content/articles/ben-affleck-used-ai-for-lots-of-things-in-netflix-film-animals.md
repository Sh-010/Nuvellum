## Artificial intelligence in production

Ben Affleck revealed that he used artificial intelligence for "lots of things" during the creation of his latest project, *Animals*. Speaking at a Bloomberg Screentime Q&A, the two-time Oscar winner discussed incorporating the technology into the kidnapping thriller, which premiered on October 9 in select theaters and on Netflix.

Affleck co-wrote, directed, and starred in the film alongside Kerry Washington. He expressed hesitation about detailing the exact applications of the technology during the filmmaking process, comparing the revelation to spoiling a magic trick by explaining how it works.

## Post-production efficiency

Despite keeping specific details under wraps, Affleck noted that the technology played a significant role during post-production. He stated that it allowed the team to move more quickly and accomplish tasks that otherwise would not have been possible.

Describing the project, Affleck emphasized that it functions as a human-centric story rather than a space-faring or heavy visual-effects spectacle. The film follows a Los Angeles mayoral candidate, played by Affleck, whose son is kidnapped.

## Corporate background

The release follows Netflix's acquisition of InterPositive, an artificial intelligence firm founded by Affleck in 2022. Affleck served as the company's CEO prior to the purchase and transitioned into a senior advisor role after the transaction concluded.
