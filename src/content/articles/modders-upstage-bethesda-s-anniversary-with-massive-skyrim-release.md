## Quiet anniversary from Bethesda

Marking 15 years since the original release of the landmark role-playing game *The Elder Scrolls 5: Skyrim*, developer Bethesda offered limited official engagement for the milestone. Celebrations included a game sale, a collector's item raffle, and minor fan-created and studio-approved content. Meanwhile, development progress on the long-awaited sequel, *The Elder Scrolls 6*, has quietly disappointed fans since its announcement eight years ago.

## Beyond Bruma 2.0 release

While official ports and updates slowed, the volunteer community stepped in with a major expansion. On Nov. 10, 2026, the creators of the *Beyond Bruma* mod released a comprehensive 2.0 overhaul. 

The updated expansion brings numerous additions, including fresh enemies, dungeons featuring diverse interiors and puzzles, new weapons like Steel Spears, spells, enchantments, and a reworked weather system. Crafters can utilize new ingredients at alchemy stations and Daedric shrines, while explorers will find 30 new books, additional paintings, and newly integrated voiced quests and cutscenes cut from the original release.

## Bringing the mod to consoles

Previously limited to personal computers since its 2017 launch and millions of downloads, the influential expansion is expanding its reach. For the first time, *Beyond Bruma* will be accessible on PlayStation consoles through Bethesda's Creation menu.

Developed entirely by volunteers at no cost, the project requires players to start a fresh save file rather than reusing previous progress.
