Nearly a decade after the conclusion of *Pretty Little Liars*, core cast members have expressed interest in returning to the teen mystery franchise.

## Cast readiness and scheduling

In an interview with Entertainment Tonight, actor Lucy Hale indicated that the former ensemble of the Freeform series would be open to reprising their roles. Hale noted that lining up a potential revival depends primarily on actor availability and the development of a suitable script and storyline.
