## Major fire at CFB Wainwright

A major fire has broken out at a military base in the Canadian province of Alberta, following what Prime Minister Mark Carney described as a "serious incident."

Carney stated in a post on X that a "serious incident creating a major fire has occurred at CFB Wainwright, in Alberta." He added that the federal government is working in coordination with Alberta officials and local authorities.

## Official responses and ongoing details

Alberta Premier Danielle Smith characterized the blaze as "significant and tragic," noting that numerous military personnel are affected.

At the time of reporting, neither Carney nor Smith had released specific details regarding the cause of the fire or whether any injuries had occurred. Carney expressed support in a social media message, stating that "our thoughts are with all the brave women and men affected."

The incident took place shortly before an upcoming October 19 referendum in Alberta regarding the province's potential separation from Canada.
