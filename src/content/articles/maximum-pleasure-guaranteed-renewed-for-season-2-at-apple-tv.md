Apple TV has renewed the series “Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed” for a second season. Created by David J. Rosen, who also serves as showrunner, the show is produced by Apple Studios and Counterpart Studios.

## Plot and Cast

The series stars Tatiana Maslany as Paula, a newly divorced mother navigating a custody battle and identity crisis. According to the official logline, Paula falls into a rabbit hole of blackmail, murder, and youth soccer after becoming convinced she witnessed a crime. Her private investigation aims to uncover a wider conspiracy while helping her rebuild her family and sense of self.

The cast features Jake Johnson as Paula’s ex-husband Karl, alongside Murray Bartlett, Dolly De Leon, Brandon Flynn, Jessy Hodges, Jon Michael Hill, Charlie Hall, Kiarra Hamagami Goldberg, and Nola Wallace.

## Production and Creative Direction

Executive producers on the project include Rosen, director David Gordon Green, Simon Kinberg, Audrey Chon, and Bard Dorros of Anonymous Content, who developed the project.

Commenting on the renewal, Rosen stated that the series title originated from a spam email and expressed gratitude to Apple and the cast and crew.

Discussing the narrative arc following the Season 1 finale, Rosen explained that the finale's twist was designed as a reframing of the protagonist. While audiences viewed Paula as a reliable narrator throughout her eccentric actions, Rosen noted that the conclusion reveals her to be a more complex character.
