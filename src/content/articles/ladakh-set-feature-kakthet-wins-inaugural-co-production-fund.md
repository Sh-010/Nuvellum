Stenzin Tankyong's debut feature film, "Kakthet" (Idiot), has won the first feature film co-production fund launched by Humans of Cinema and Safarnaama Pictures. Set in Ladakh, the Ladakhi-language project was chosen from nearly 400 submissions following an evaluation process. 

## Plot and Production Background

The narrative centers on a government school and its surrounding community facing an uncertain future tied to a reluctant tenth-grade student who has never passed an exam. Teachers must try to save the institution through this single student. Tankyong has spent eight years creating films highlighting Ladakh's culture and landscapes, with previous short films screening at international festivals.

Producers Ritu Sarin and Rajesh Thanickan are attached to the project alongside French co-producers La Belle Affaire Productions. Prior to winning the fund, the project secured the Co-Production Features Grant at the 2025 WAVES Film Bazaar and was selected for the 2026 Asian Project Market at the Busan International Film Festival.

## Mentors and Jury Support

The fund was founded by Harshit Bansal of Humans of Cinema and Nazim Momin of Safarnaama Pictures. Filmmakers Vishal Bhardwaj and Shaunak Sen served as mentors, with Sen also taking on an executive producer role. The selection jury included actor Imran Khan, filmmaker Arati Kadav, producer Aman Mann, and festival director Aseem Chhabra.

Bansal noted the high volume of projects received from across the country, emphasizing the need for such initiatives in South Asia. Tankyong expressed gratitude for the domestic support, highlighting the difficulty independent regional filmmakers face in securing backing for localized stories. 

The screenplay is currently in its fourth draft. The production team completed location scouting in Leh and the Changthang region, and is currently focused on locking down additional financing and international co-production partnerships.
