## Attorney statements and hospital status

Christa Pike was unconscious and on a ventilator in a Nashville hospital following a botched execution attempt, according to a court filing submitted by her attorneys on Friday. Pike was audibly snoring after receiving two doses of a lethal injection on Wednesday.

Her lawyers stated in the court filing that they believe issues with the intravenous lines caused the lethal drug, pentobarbital, to enter her body tissue rather than her bloodstream. According to the legal team, the execution team did not recognize that the IV lines were incorrectly placed or that the veins had blown, and proceeded to prepare and administer a second dose of pentobarbital instead of addressing the failure.

## Background and reactions

Pike has been on death row following her 1996 conviction and death sentence for the 1995 murder of 19-year-old Colleen Slemmer. She was 50 years old and set to become the first woman executed in Tennessee in 200 years.

Following the incident, Tennessee Governor Bill Lee temporarily paused the state execution on Thursday and announced that a comprehensive, third-party review of the case would take place. Questions remain regarding future legal steps and whether the state can attempt another execution given Pike's current medical condition.

According to the Death Penalty Information Center, Pike is believed to be the first person to survive the administration of lethal drugs in the modern era of the death penalty.
