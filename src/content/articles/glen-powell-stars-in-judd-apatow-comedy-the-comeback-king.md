Universal Pictures has debuted the trailer for "The Comeback King," a new comedy directed by Judd Apatow. The film stars Glen Powell as a struggling country music star trying to revive his damaged reputation.

## Plot and Musical Cameos

In the film, Powell plays a musician whose career collapses after his pop star partner, portrayed by Madelyn Cline, falsely accuses him of infidelity. To orchestrate a professional recovery, his character enlists assistance from prominent country music figures, including Miranda Lambert, Reba McEntire, Jelly Roll, Brad Paisley, Chris Stapleton, Megan Moroney, and Shaboozey. Powell and Cline also perform original songs in the picture.

## Cast and Crew

Apatow and Powell co-wrote the screenplay. The project represents Apatow's first directorial feature since his 2022 pandemic comedy "The Bubble." The ensemble cast further includes Cristin Milioti, Stavros Halkias, and Jin Hao Li. Universal Pictures has scheduled the film for a theatrical release on February 5.
