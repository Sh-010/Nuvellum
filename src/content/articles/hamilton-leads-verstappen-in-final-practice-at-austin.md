Lewis Hamilton clocked the fastest time in the final practice session for the United States Grand Prix in Austin, finishing narrowly ahead of Max Verstappen. Hamilton outperformed Verstappen by 0.168 seconds, setting the stage for a competitive qualifying session as Ferrari and Red Bull position themselves to contend for pole position.

## Practice Incidents and Track Limits

Charles Leclerc had initially set the fastest time during the session, but his lap was deleted due to track limits. Despite this, Leclerc secured third place overall. Track limits emerged as a notable concern for drivers due to the circuit's tarmac run-off areas. Lando Norris also had a fast lap deleted for exceeding track limits, leaving the McLaren driver in eighth place, while his teammate Oscar Piastri took sixth.

## Contenders and Traffic Challenges

Championship leader Kimi Antonelli finished three tenths behind Hamilton's leading pace, placing fourth, with his Mercedes teammate George Russell fifth. Anthony Davidson noted that Ferrari's speed appears genuine following Friday's sessions. Meanwhile, traffic caused disruptions on the track, as Hamilton avoided Gabriel Bortoleto and Isack Hadjar expressed frustration regarding traffic involving Alex Albon.
