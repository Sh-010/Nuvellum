Netflix's upcoming live-action adaptation of the Gundam franchise has remained largely under wraps since development began in 2018. However, cast member Noah Centineo recently shared initial plot details during an appearance on the Happy Sad Confused podcast.

## A galactic romance

Centineo described the project as a major sci-fi romance, characterizing the narrative as a space-faring romance between characters on opposing sides of a generational conflict. This premise aligns with the official synopsis released for the film, which outlines a story centered on shifting allegiances, high-stakes interstellar conflict, and a race that could determine the fate of humanity.

Additionally, Centineo confirmed that the film is set within the franchise's Universal Century timeline. This foundational era hosts many of the most recognizable entries in the Gundam universe, though the specific placement within the timeline remains unannounced.

## Cast and production status

Directed by Jim Mickle, the movie features an ensemble cast alongside Centineo and Sydney Sweeney. Confirmed cast members include Jason Isaacs, Michael Mando, Jackson White, Shioli Kutsuna, Nonso Anozie, Javon Walton, Oleksandr Rudynskyi, Ida Brooke, and Gemma Chua-Tran.

Filming concluded in July, though Netflix has not yet announced a formal release date for the project.
