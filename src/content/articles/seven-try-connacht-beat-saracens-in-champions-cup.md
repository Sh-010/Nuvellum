## Match overview

Connacht delivered a strong attacking display to defeat Saracens 45-36 in their opening Investec Champions Cup pool game at Dexcom Stadium. Captain Cian Prendergast and fly-half Ciaran Frawley each scored two tries as Connacht crossed the line seven times against the three-time Champions Cup winners. 

Saracens had an opportunity to secure a losing bonus point, but Owen Farrell's conversion following Elliot Daly's late try missed its target.

## First-half action

Saracens started strongly, building an early 12-0 lead through tries from Sione Vaenuku and Noah Caluori. Connacht responded by scoring 19 unanswered points, with Prendergast, Cathal Forde, and Shamus Hurley-Langton all touching down to give the hosts the lead.

Just before halftime, Hurley-Langton received a yellow card. Saracens capitalized immediately when Nick Tompkins scored from close range as the clock entered the red, leaving the halftime score level at 19-19.

## Second-half decider

Playing with 14 men early in the second half, Connacht struck first when Bundee Aki set up Prendergast for his second try. Saracens answered quickly as Farrell and Daly created an easy run-in for Rotimi Segun.

Frawley scored twice and replacement Caolin Blade added another to establish a 14-point advantage for Connacht. Although Vaenuku scored his second try for the visitors, Connacht maintained their lead to secure the victory. A bonus point eluded Connacht under tournament rules requiring teams to score three or more tries than their opponents.

In another Pool Two fixture on Saturday, La Rochelle defeated the South African side Lions 31-14.
