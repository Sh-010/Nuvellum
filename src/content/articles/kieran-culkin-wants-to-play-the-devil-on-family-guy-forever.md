Actor Kieran Culkin enjoyed voicing the Devil in an upcoming *Family Guy* Halloween special so much that he intends to return to the role permanently, according to the show's creators.

## A permanent return to hell

Co-showrunner Alex Sulkin stated that after recording for the episode, Culkin insisted on continuing to voice the character if the animated series revisits the Devil. Sulkin noted that the creative team will gladly accept his offer.

The animated special, titled "Happy Hell-o-Ween," is scheduled to air October 5 on Hulu. In the episode, characters Stewie and Brian choose to attend a Halloween party instead of saving a drowning man, leading to their demise and arrival in purgatory. They ultimately end up in the office of the Devil, voiced by Culkin, who is depicted as overwhelmed by his position.

## Production process and casting

Co-showrunner Richard Appel noted that Culkin is a fan of the program and participated enthusiastically, observing that guest actors on the show receive guild minimum pay rather than large sums. Sulkin, who directed Culkin's recording sessions, remarked that the actor's energy closely mirrored his role as Roman Roy in the television series *Succession*.

Appel explained that each *Family Guy* episode requires approximately 14 months to complete, from initial table reads to final edits, which prevents the production from covering immediate current events. Despite the lengthy timeline, the showrunners introduced unexpected figures into their depiction of Hell for comedic effect.
