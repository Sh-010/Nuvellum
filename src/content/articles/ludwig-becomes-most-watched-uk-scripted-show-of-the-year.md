The comedy-drama *Ludwig*, produced by Big Talk Studios for the BBC and BritBox, has reached 9.1 million viewers for the first episode of its second season, making it the UK's most-watched scripted launch of the year so far. According to Barb data from overnights.tv cited by the BBC, the milestone places the show ahead of rival scripted broadcasts and streamers.

## Viewership milestones and comparisons

The figures place *Ludwig* ahead of other prominent scripted titles, including Prime Video’s *The Night Manager* Season 2, which reached 8.7 million viewers, and the BBC comedy-drama *Small Prophets*, which drew 7.6 million. However, on the unscripted side, *Ludwig* remains behind another BBC programme, *The Traitors*, which earlier in the year achieved a consolidated audience of 13.3 million.

While the second season concluded with 1.6 million overnight viewers, the series accumulated its higher numbers through catch-up viewing on BBC iPlayer. Its first season previously broke out as the BBC’s top-rated scripted launch of 2024.

## Cast, creators, and future episodes

Created by Mark Brotherhood and produced by Big Talk, *Ludwig* stars David Mitchell as John Taylor, a reclusive puzzle maker who investigates cases after impersonating his missing twin brother at a local police station. The cast also features Anna Maxwell Martin and Izuka Hoyle.

Big Talk boss Kenton Allen described the series as a "gloriously eccentric detective at the heart of a show that has never played by the usual rules." The programme has already received a recommission for a third season, as well as a Christmas special featuring David Suchet.
