## Golden horse nominations announced

Sabu’s film *Arrested Memory* has secured 11 nominations, leading the field for the 63rd Golden Horse Awards. Shu Qi’s *Girl* followed closely behind with 10 nominations, while Joseph Hsu Chen-chieh’s *Double Happiness* and Yeung Leung-chuen’s *We Are Born Good* each received nine nominations. 

All four features are nominated for best narrative feature and best film editing, alongside Anthony Chen’s *We Are All Strangers*. Each of the top four films also secured a nominee in either the best director or best new director categories.

## Directing and acting categories

In the best director race, Sabu, Hsu, and Chen are joined by Herman Yau for *We’re Nothing at All* and Edmund Yeo for *The Age of Goodbyes*. The best new director category features Shu and Yeung competing against Priscilla Ang Geck-geck for *Ah Girl*, Shieh Meng-ju for *Mudborn*, and Michelle Zhou for *Big Little Things*.

Ethan Juan received a best leading actor nomination for his work in *Arrested Memory*, competing against Leon Dai (*Kung Fu*), Liu Kuan-ting (*Double Happiness*), Yeung Wai-lun (*We Are Born Good*), and Jack Tan (*Dead Tide*).

The best leading actress contenders are Yeo Yann-yann (*We Are All Strangers*), Angela Yuen (*Afterpiece*), Jennifer Yu (*Double Happiness*), Liang Cuishan (*Big Little Things*), and Chung Suet-ying (*We Are Born Good*).

## Festival and ceremony details

Organizers reviewed 587 entries for this year's event, which included 92 narrative features, 60 feature documentaries, three animated features, 294 live-action shorts, 71 documentary shorts, and 67 animated shorts. Ma Shih-yuan and Jimmy Liu announced the nominations.

Wen Tien-hsiang, CEO of the Taipei Golden Horse Film Festival Executive Committee, announced colorist Moya Chou as the Outstanding Taiwanese Filmmaker of the Year, and Yu Kan-ping as the lifetime achievement award honoree.

Every nominated film will screen during the Golden Horse Film Festival, which opens on November 6. The awards ceremony is scheduled for November 22 at the Taipei Music Center. Starting with this edition, the ceremony will take place on a Sunday instead of a Saturday.
