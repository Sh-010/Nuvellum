Seven years after the release of *Once Upon a Time in Hollywood*, Quentin Tarantino's period action-drama is receiving an official sequel. Rather than being directed by Tarantino, the direct sequel is helmed by *Fight Club* and *Zodiac* director David Fincher.

## Cast and plot details

The film, titled *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth* (previously known as *The Adventures of Cliff Booth*), centers on Brad Pitt's stuntman character Cliff Booth. Pitt reprises his Academy Award-winning Best Supporting Actor role from the original 2019 film.

While the original movie was set in 1969, Netflix states that the sequel takes place in 1977 in a "very different Hollywood." Alongside Pitt, the cast includes Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Carla Gugino, Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, Peter Weller, Matt Groove, JB Tadena, Corey Fogelmanis, and Karren Karagulian. Fincher directed the film from a script written by Tarantino.

## Release schedule

Audiences will have opportunities to view the film starting in November. Netflix has scheduled an exclusive two-week run in IMAX theaters beginning Nov. 25, ahead of the movie's streaming debut on Netflix on Dec. 23.

Additionally, HarperCollins is publishing a novelization written by Tarantino titled *The Adventures of Cliff Booth* on Tuesday, Dec. 8.
