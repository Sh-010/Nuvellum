## Suspension announced by BBC Studios

Professional dancer Val Chmerkovskiy has been suspended from *Dancing with the Stars* for one week following a social media post that appeared to reference Will Smith's 2022 Academy Awards altercation with Chris Rock.

In a statement, BBC Studios stated that the well-being of everyone working on the program is non-negotiable. The organization described the social media post as inappropriate and stated it did not reflect the values or standards expected of individuals connected to the production. Consequently, Chmerkovskiy will not participate in the upcoming week's episode.

## Context of the controversy

The controversy began following the program's first "Super Bowl Night," during which judge Carrie Ann Inaba offered a critical review of a performance by Chmerkovskiy's wife, fellow professional dancer Jenna Johnson. In a since-deleted TikTok post, Chmerkovskiy wrote, "You almost saw Fresh Prince enter the ballroom last night."

The comment alluded to the incident at the 2022 Oscars where Will Smith struck Chris Rock after a joke directed at Smith's wife, Jada Pinkett Smith.

## Apology and partner arrangements

Following the backlash, Chmerkovskiy issued an apology on Instagram, stating he reached out to Carrie Ann Inaba to apologize directly and personally, while also offering a public apology. He expressed regret for making the reference and acknowledged the responsibilities of having a platform.

Chmerkovskiy's celebrity partner, Jenna Dewan, is scheduled to perform next week with professional dancer Alan Bersten in a routine dedicated to her mother.
