## From early music fame to prison

Ashley Walters achieved early success in his youth as a member of the So Solid Crew and a solo music artist, but his early life also involved criminal activity and legal trouble. Before turning 21, he served a seven-month prison sentence following an arrest in 2002. Reflecting on that period, Walters noted that his time behind bars prompted a major turning point, leading him to reevaluate his life choices and social circle.

Following his release, his music career stalled and travel restrictions limited his opportunities. However, filmmaker Saul Dibb cast him in the 2004 film *Bullet Boy*, helping launch a successful acting career that later included prominent roles in television dramas such as *Top Boy* and *Adolescence*.

## Directing *Animol* and addressing masculinity

At age 44, Walters has expanded his career into filmmaking by directing his debut feature, *Animol*. Set in a young offender institution, the film draws partially on his own memories of incarceration, though it is not strictly autobiographical. The movie explores themes of fear, shame, trauma, and societal pressures regarding masculinity, which Walters identifies as recurring subjects in his work.

He has also spoken openly about his past struggles with alcohol and cocaine addiction, which lasted for about 15 years before he entered rehabilitation in November 2020. Having maintained his sobriety since, Walters relocated from London to the Kent coast and credits the lifestyle change with transforming his personal and professional life.
