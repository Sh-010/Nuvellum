## Narrative depth and world exploration

During an interview at the 2026 Paris Game Week, Final Fantasy 7 remake trilogy director Naoki Hamaguchi discussed upcoming details regarding the final installment, *Final Fantasy 7 Revelation*, slated for release in April 2027. Set against an impending planetary catastrophe, the narrative emphasizes existential themes rather than a frantic rush to stop Sephiroth. Developers integrated side quests and non-player character interactions directly into the experience, encouraging players to explore the world deeply.

To support large-scale travel, the development team engineered a seamless map-loading system. When players descend from the Highwind, the game initially displays low-resolution assets across a wide view before loading high-detail data dynamically as characters approach, discarding distant assets to maintain optimal performance. Additionally, the game features a Dynamic Mode that scales enemies to match player levels, ensuring balanced progression regardless of how much optional content is completed.

## Combat systems and loadout flexibility

Battle mechanics feature new complexities through the optional FITS system, which assigns traits altering character battle styles. According to Hamaguchi and battle director Teruki Endo, extensive playtesting confirms the game remains beatable whether participants utilize the system or bypass it entirely.

Players can configure up to five customizable loadout presets per character, adjusting weapons, equipment, and materia for specific combat encounters. These configurations can be swapped between battles to provide tactical specialization and flexibility.

## Future development horizons

Following the conclusion of the trilogy, Hamaguchi expressed interest in continuing work with the development team. Potential future directions include developing the next series installment, creating a new AAA intellectual property, or leading another remake project, though Hamaguchi noted that subsequent remakes might be better suited for other teams.
