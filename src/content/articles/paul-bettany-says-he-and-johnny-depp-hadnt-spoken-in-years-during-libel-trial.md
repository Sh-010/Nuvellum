## Comments on the trial

In a recent interview with The Times, actor Paul Bettany addressed public perceptions following a libel lawsuit brought by Johnny Depp against The Sun. Bettany discussed text message exchanges between the two actors that were read in court during a February 2020 trial hearing.

“I hadn’t spoken to Johnny in years when that happened and I haven’t spoken to him since,” Bettany told the publication. “I was being painted as this close friend of a guy I hadn’t spoken to in years and, honestly, amid all the shit two celebrities threw at each other, that bit was the most odd.”

Bettany added that he decided against making public statements at the time, noting that people who know him understood his character. The actors previously collaborated on multiple films, including *The Tourist* (2010), *Transcendence* (2014), and *Mortdecai* (2015).

## The text messages and court case

The text messages made public during the proceedings included a 2013 exchange in which Depp wrote, “Let’s burn Amber!!!” Bettany replied: “Having thought it through I don’t think we should burn Amber – she’s delightful company and easy on the eye, plus I’m not sure she’s a witch. We could of course try the English course of action in these predicaments – we do a drowning test. Thoughts?” Depp responded that they should “drown her before we burn her!!! I will fuck her burnt corpse afterwards to make sure she’s dead.”

Depp initially filed a libel lawsuit against News Group Newspapers, publisher of The Sun, regarding an article titled “Gone Potty – How can JK Rowling be ‘genuinely happy’ casting wife beater Johnny Depp in the new ‘Fantastic Beasts’ film?” Depp argued the publication caused significant distress and harm to his reputation. In 2020, Judge Andrew Nicol delivered a ruling against Depp.
