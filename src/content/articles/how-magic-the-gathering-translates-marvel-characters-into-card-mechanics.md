## Capturing character through mechanics

Magic: The Gathering features crossover sets under the Universes Beyond line, bringing fictional worlds into the card game. Designing these sets involves translating the traits, powers, and stories of well-known characters directly into gameplay rules, abilities, and restrictions, sometimes replacing the need for traditional exposition or flavor text.

## Translating heroes and villains

Several cards in the Marvel Super Heroes set and related superdrops utilize specific mechanics to reflect their subjects:

*   **Age of Ultron:** Designed as a five-mana Saga enchantment, the card represents an entire comic crossover event by purging biological life, producing Robot tokens, and granting synthetic forces deathtouch and +1/+1 counters.
*   **Wolverine, Fierce Fighter:** The card's rules specify that whenever new damage would be dealt to Wolverine, all previously marked damage is wiped clean, simulating his mutant healing factor and continuous recovery in combat.
*   **Storm, Force of Nature:** Released in a Secret Lair superdrop, this Omega-level mutant grants the Storm mechanic to the next instant or sorcery spell cast after she deals combat damage to a player.
*   **Mjölnir, Hammer of Thor:** To respect the lore that the hammer can only be lifted by the worthy, the card features an "Equip worthy" mechanic that restricts it to legendary non-Villain creatures with red or white in their color identity.
*   **Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk:** A double-faced card representing the character's internal conflict. The front face functions as a fragile scientist who draws cards, while the back face is an 8/8 force that gains a +1/+1 counter, untaps, and grants an additional combat phase when taking damage.
*   **Doctor Doom, King of Latveria:** This card turns discarded land cards into life loss for opponents and grants Villains menace and the ability to connive, reflecting a calculating sovereign who manipulates others.
*   **Captain America, Living Legend:** Features vigilance and a secondary rule that untaps a creature when it becomes tapped for the first time during a turn, representing a hero who rallies his team.
*   **Molecule Man:** Grants nonland cards in hand the Miracle keyword, allowing players to cast spells for zero mana when drawn as the first card of the turn, echoing his physical matter manipulation.
*   **The Mind Stone:** An indestructible artifact that generates white mana and, after a five-mana activation cost, lets players blink another nonland permanent at the beginning of their end step.

## Tactical locations and infrastructure

Land cards in the set also adapt comic book geography into active tactical hubs. *Avengers Tower* functions as a rally point that filters mana for Hero spells and allows players to recruit teammates from their library, while *Castle Doom* generates Doombot tokens to reward aggressive play.
