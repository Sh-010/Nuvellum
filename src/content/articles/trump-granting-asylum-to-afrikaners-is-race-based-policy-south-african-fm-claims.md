Speaking on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York, South African Foreign Minister Ronald Lamola addressed Washington's planned programme to take in up to 17,500 additional White South Africans. 

## Asylum policy and genocide claims

In an interview, Lamola characterized the United States policy as "a race-based policy". He additionally dismissed claims regarding a "so-called genocide" of Afrikaners, referring to such assertions as "fake news". However, the foreign minister stated that Pretoria would "not interfere" with the American programme.

## International Court of Justice case and regional violence

Lamola also addressed foreign policy and domestic security issues during his remarks. He maintained that South Africa continues to stand by its genocide case against Israel at the International Court of Justice concerning the conflict in Gaza, despite pressure from the Trump administration to drop the legal action.

Additionally, Lamola responded to accusations from Nigeria regarding recent anti-foreigner violence in South Africa, describing the characterisation as "very unfortunate". He maintained that the attacks were not state-sponsored, but rather resulted from the activities of vigilante groups.
