## Police decision on social media post

Police in the United Kingdom have determined that a social media post by the wife of Prime Minister Andy Burnham’s national security adviser that appears to endorse Palestine Action does not merit a criminal investigation. 

A Metropolitan Police spokesperson stated that officers examined Sarah Helm’s post and ruled that it did not meet the criminal threshold, adding that no further police action would be taken. Officers had previously stated on Friday that the post was being assessed to determine if an investigation was required.

## Context of the online comments

Helm, an author and journalist married to National Security Adviser Jonathan Powell, wrote on X on Thursday: “God help us authors who support Palestine and Palestine Action.” 

Her comment followed an announcement by publisher Faber that it could no longer supply Irish author Sally Rooney's works in the UK. Faber stated that current UK legislation prevented it from passing on UK earnings to Rooney after the author announced plans to use royalties to support Palestine Action.

The British NGO Campaign Against Antisemitism responded on X by calling Helm's post "gobsmacking" and arguing that the police needed to investigate. A government spokesperson subsequently stated that Helm’s comments did not represent the government in any capacity.

## Legal status of Palestine Action

Palestine Action was proscribed as a terrorist organisation following a vote by UK lawmakers in July 2025. 

According to Amnesty International, more than 3,500 people have been arrested in the UK for allegedly supporting Palestine Action since the ban, often for holding placards. While the group challenged the government's decision, the Court of Appeal ruled the proscription lawful in June.
