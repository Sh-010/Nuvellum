## Streaming departure on Dec. 1

Netflix subscribers have a limited window remaining to watch Ridley Scott's 1979 science fiction horror classic, *Alien*. The film is scheduled to leave the platform on Dec. 1, alongside its acclaimed sequel, *Aliens*, directed by James Cameron. The simultaneous departure marks a final opportunity for audiences to view the foundational entries of the franchise on the streaming service.

## A defining sci-fi horror franchise

Released originally in 1979, *Alien* follows a commercial spacecraft crew investigating a mysterious distress signal on a nearby moon. The narrative pivots from an apparent medical emergency to a survival nightmare after a hostile extraterrestrial organism boards the vessel and hunts the crew members sequentially. 

The film starred Sigourney Weaver as Ellen Ripley and established a multi-decade franchise spanning various media. Subsequent entries have included prequels exploring Xenomorph origins, the 2024 release *Alien: Romulus*, and related video game adaptations such as *Alien: Rogue Incursion*. Following their removal from Netflix, viewers will need to utilize alternative streaming providers to access the two films.
