Munster started their Champions Cup campaign with a 33-25 victory over Racing 92 on an emotional night at Pairc Ui Chaoimh. The fixture was played a decade and a day after the passing of Munster legend Anthony Foley, the first Munster captain to lift the Heineken Cup, who died in his sleep prior to a scheduled match between the same two teams in October 2016.

Before kick-off in Cork, the Shannon RFC choir performed *There is an Isle*, a club song featured at Foley's Limerick funeral. Neither side fielded a player wearing Foley's number eight jersey, and a minute's applause was held in the eighth minute before members of the former Ireland back row's family.

## First-half advantage

Racing 92 opened the scoring through an early Antoine Gibert penalty, but Munster responded strongly in the first half. Brian Gleeson, wearing number 24 in the absence of the usual number eight shirt, crossed from the base of a scrum after six minutes. Shay McCarthy then added a second try after 13 minutes following a fortuitous bounce. Tadhg Beirne extended Munster's lead early in the second half by crashing over from close range, giving the hosts a 15-point cushion.

## Racing comeback and late sealing try

Despite trailing significantly, Racing 92 narrowed the deficit to three points in the second half through tries from Wilfried Hulleu, Maxime Machenaud, and Joey Manu, with Fineen Wycherley scoring for Munster during that period. 

Arthur Roche received a late yellow card for a clumsy collision with Munster full-back Ben O'Connor, hampering the French side's momentum. Craig Casey subsequently sealed the 33-25 win for Munster with a try from the back of a maul two minutes before the final whistle.
