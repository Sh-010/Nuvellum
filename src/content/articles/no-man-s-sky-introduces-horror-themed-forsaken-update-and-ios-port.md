## Exploring the darker side of the universe

Hello Games has launched a seasonal horror-themed patch for No Man's Sky titled Forsaken. The 7.10 update encourages players to investigate the darker side of the game by tracking down haunted technology and biological horrors. Adventurers must scavenge derelict hulks, abandoned freighters, planetary dig sites, and infested outposts to collect strange mementos.

These items can be traded with enigmatic collectors for biomechanical corvette modules, new cockpits, and unsettling ship interiors. Rewards also include a larva tank helmet and a weapon style described by the developer as a wriggling grub cannon multi-tool.

## Quality-of-life additions and mobile launch

For players less interested in the frightening content, the patch introduces general improvements such as seamless warping to nearby stars through distant constellations without opening the map. The update also adds galactic postal missions, a weapon selection radial menu, and a minimal heads-up display mode.

Simultaneously, an iOS port for iPhone and iPad has arrived, featuring all accumulated updates including Forsaken. Users on iPhone 11 and newer can experience the initial portion of the game at no cost, utilizing custom touch controls designed for mobile hardware.

## A decade of development

Marking the tenth anniversary year of No Man's Sky, Forsaken represents the fifth major content release following patches like Remnant and Cosmos. Studio founder Sean Murray noted that the team is already planning additional content for the year 2027.
