Steam Next Fest is set to return for its October edition, offering players a chance to sample free demos for upcoming PC games. The upcoming event follows previous editions held earlier in the year in February and June.

## Event Schedule and Details

The PC demo showcase will run from Oct. 19 at 1:00 p.m. EDT to Oct. 26. During the multi-day event, independent and major developers upload free trials of their upcoming titles to the Steam store, allowing users to test out a wide variety of unreleased games before their official launches.

## Featured Demos and Early Access

While the official event kicks off next week, several demos are already accessible to players on the platform, including *Ace & Aro: Heart-to-Heart* and *Fallgrade*. 

Other confirmed titles slated to appear during the October showcase include *Emberville*, described as a riff on *Stardew Valley*, the drinking game *Drunken Goddess Reflux*, and the multiplayer body-hiding game *We Are So Cooked*. Certain featured titles have already accumulated significant attention on the platform prior to the festival's official launch.
