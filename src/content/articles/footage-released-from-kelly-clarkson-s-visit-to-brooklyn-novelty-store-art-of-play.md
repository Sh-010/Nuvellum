## Store visit footage revealed

Surveillance and alternative-angle footage documenting Kelly Clarkson’s visit to Art of Play, a Brooklyn novelty store, has been acquired and released by Pablo Torre, host and executive editor of the investigative podcast “Pablo Torre Finds Out.” 

The publication of the video adds visual context to an ongoing public dispute between the singer and the local business. Clarkson previously criticized the store on social media, alleging she received dismissive treatment comparable to the film *Pretty Woman*. According to the singer, a store employee looked at her as though she could not afford a $15,000 bird automaton named Molly. 

## Contrasting accounts of the interaction

The released footage shows Clarkson browsing the shop alongside her children while wearing a baseball hat, a bright yellow T-shirt, baggy jeans, and a backpack. In the video, she approaches the bird automaton and interacts with a store employee who points toward the display. 

The two parties offered conflicting explanations of the exchange. Art of Play stated publicly that the bird is permanently attached to the wall, plays a role in store magic shows, and is not for sale. Clarkson disputed this narrative on social media, maintaining that a price tag hung on the wall and that the employee told her the item cost $15,000 while offering to find a comparable alternative.

The footage shows Clarkson parting ways with the employee as he assists other customers. She then returns items she was holding, speaks briefly and gesticulates to an employee behind the counter, and leaves. Her entire visit lasted less than five minutes, with her initial social media post published 15 minutes after her departure.

## Impact on the business

Store owner Adam Rubin stated in an interview that the business has faced severe repercussions since Clarkson named the store online. According to Rubin, the shop has been review-bombed, and employees and surrounding businesses have experienced harassment and safety concerns.

Rubin suggested that Clarkson may have felt she received subpar treatment because the store was crowded and she was dressed casually, noting that staff treated her like a normal customer rather than meeting expectations for special treatment.
