## Recognition at the 36th annual ceremony

The cast and creative team of the biographical film "Michael" are set to receive the Gotham Musical Tribute at the 36th Annual Gotham Film Awards Ceremony. The honorees include director Antoine Fuqua, producer Graham King, and actors Colman Domingo, Jaafar Jackson, and Nia Long. 

The award marks the second Gotham Musical Tribute, following last year's inaugural presentation. Jeffrey Sharp, the executive director of the Gotham Film and Media Institute, stated that the award recognizes the filmmakers and actors for bringing Michael Jackson's life, legend, and music to the screen, noting that their work captures the humanity behind the artist.

## About the film and event

"Michael" follows the musician's journey from The Jackson Five to his career as an influential solo artist, exploring his life both on and off the stage. The film stars Jaafar Jackson as Michael Jackson alongside KeiLyn Durrel Jones, Laura Harrier, Juliano Krue Valdi, and Miles Teller. It was written by John Logan and produced by Graham King, John Branca, and John McClain.

According to the announcement, the film has grossed more than $1 billion globally and holds the record as the highest-grossing biographical film of all time, with a sequel currently in development. 

The tribute will be presented on November 30 at Cipriani Wall Street in New York City. Additional honorees at this year's event include Danny Boyle, who will receive the Gotham Director Tribute for "Ink," "La Bola Negra" receiving the Gotham Vanguard Tribute, and Laika Studios receiving the Gotham Anniversary Tribute. Nominees for the awards will be announced on October 27.
