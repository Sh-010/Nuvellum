Filmmaker Rian Johnson has partnered with longtime cinematographer Steve Yedlin to release a new iPhone photography application called Chromagraph Camera. 

## Bringing Cinematic Processing to Mobile

Announced by Johnson on social media, the application shoots RAW images while bypassing standard iPhone look processing. Instead, the tool applies the same image processing methods the duo uses on feature films like *Brick*, *Star Wars: Episode VIII – The Last Jedi*, and the *Knives Out* series, including the recent installment *Wake Up Dead Man*.

Johnson stated on X that he encouraged his cinematographer to build his ideal mobile still camera application. He described it as a streamlined option that utilizes Yedlin's custom processing techniques.

## Features and Limitations

Users can adjust images within the application using color, exposure, grain, and halation tools before exporting them directly to their camera roll. 

Speaking with Wired, Yedlin clarified that the application is not meant to replace Apple's native Camera app, but rather serves as a curated tool that captures images according to their preferred aesthetic. He also noted that the software currently only handles still photography and does not support video capture, adding that mobile devices still lack the full control found in professional camera equipment.
