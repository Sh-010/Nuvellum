Comedian Kevin James Doyle is set to release his solo show *After Endgame: A Comedy About Chess* as his fourth comedy special. The production will premiere on his YouTube channel on February 11, 2027.

## Background and Inspiration

The show draws on Doyle's background teaching thousands of chess lessons. The narrative follows his journey to Singapore after a mysterious investor flew him out to teach chess to wealthy individuals, leading to high-stakes situations.

Doyle started doing stand-up around the same time he began teaching chess in New York City. For the stage production, he drew creative inspiration from cinematic films and sleight-of-hand magic performances.

## Tour History and Production

Following a critically acclaimed run at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe in 2024, the show traveled to several cities and completed a four-week off-Broadway run at the Soho Playhouse.

Longtime collaborator Cory Cavin directed the special. Doyle serves as Creative Producer at the Soho Playhouse, where he leads the children's theater program, and has previously released other festival shows as taped specials.
