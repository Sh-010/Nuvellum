## Shortlist categories and ceremony details

The European Film Academy has announced its shortlist of films competing for prizes at the 39th European Film Awards. The ceremony is scheduled to take place on January 16, 2027, in Athens. A total of 68 feature films have been shortlisted for potential nomination, with the final winners to be decided by the votes of the European Film Academy's members. The selected works represent 40 European countries and feature a mix of established and emerging filmmakers, including 25 films directed or co-directed by women.

## Fiction shortlist highlights

Forty-five films were selected for the fiction shortlist. Among them are Cannes 2026 Palme d’Or winner *Fjord*, Venice Golden Lion winner *Woman Unknown*, and awards season contenders such as *La Bola Negra* and *Coward*. 

Additional titles on the fiction list include Pedro Almodóvar's *Bitter Christmas*, Werner Herzog's *Bucking Fastard*, Florian Zeller's *Bunker*, and works directed by filmmakers across Europe and beyond, such as Paweł Pawlikowski, Radu Jude, Mike Leigh, and Andrey Zvyagintsev.

## Documentary and animation selections

The documentary shortlist comprises 15 titles, featuring *NAZA* and the French box office release *Whispers in the Woods*. Other documentary entries include *Citizen Osama*, *Everybody to Kenmure Street*, and *Traces*. 

The animation shortlist highlights festival titles including *In Waves*, *Iron Boy (Le Corset)*, and *Jim Queen*, alongside international co-productions such as *Decorado*, *Julián*, and *Viva Carmen*.
