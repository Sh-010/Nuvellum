## Delegation targeted in Taybeh

A delegation of European diplomats and Catholic representatives was attacked by masked Israeli settlers in the occupied West Bank. The officials had travelled to the Christian village of Taybeh, near Ramallah, to assess conditions following frequent settler attacks on Palestinians attempting to gather their olive harvest.

Video footage from the scene showed masked individuals confronting the convoy and stones being thrown. Belgian authorities reported that a car from their consulate had a window smashed, and a French official, identified as Bernard Baudoux, mayor of Aulnoye-Aymeries, was assaulted. Baudoux stated that he was kicked, beaten with iron bars, and thrown to the ground by enraged attackers.

## International reactions and calls for accountability

Belgium's foreign minister, Maxime Prevot, condemned the assault, stating that Israel has a responsibility to protect Palestinians, diplomats, and journalists, and called for EU sanctions against violent settlers and their supporters. 

Israel's foreign minister, Gideon Sa'ar, also condemned the violence by what he termed "Israeli rioters" and noted that the ministry was in contact with the relevant ambassadors, though he added that the visit had not been coordinated with the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) in advance.

The IDF condemned the violence and stated that a preliminary inquiry indicated no soldiers were present at the time, adding that an investigation is underway.

## Disputed accounts

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's office cast doubt on the reported sequence of events, urging caution and suggesting the incident was part of a "coordinated campaign to smear Israel" during the annual harvest. Netanyahu's office stated that social media images did not establish the full sequence or individual responsibility, and noted that police and forensic investigators are looking into the matter.

The incident followed a separate attack a day earlier in another West Bank village, where Palestinians and Washington Post photographer Heidi Levine were injured. That earlier assault drew criticism from US officials, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio and US Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee, who described it as a despicable act of terror carried out by masked cowards.
