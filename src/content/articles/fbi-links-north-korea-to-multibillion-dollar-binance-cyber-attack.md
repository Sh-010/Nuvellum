## FBI attributes massive crypto theft to state-linked actors

The United States Federal Bureau of Investigation stated that North Korea orchestrated a massive virtual asset heist targeting the cryptocurrency exchange Binance on October 29, 2026. The incident resulted in the theft of over $6 billion in digital assets. 

According to the bureau, the malicious cyber operation is designated as "TraderTraitor." Federal investigators, working alongside partners including the United States Secret Service and the Internal Revenue Service Criminal Investigation division, reported tracking the stolen funds as the perpetrators converted them primarily into Ethereum.

## Investigation and platform response

Authorities indicated that the actors involved are preparing the assets for further laundering stages by utilizing decentralized finance and instant swap services. The law enforcement agency provided limited additional details or underlying evidence regarding the attribution.

In the wake of the security breach, Binance temporarily froze customer withdrawals. The exchange resumed normal withdrawal operations shortly after the incident was addressed.
