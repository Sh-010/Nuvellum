## Launching Sisu Pictures

Award-winning current affairs producer-directors Gesbeen Mohammad and Robin Barnwell have established a new independent production company named Sisu Pictures. The founders, whose previous credits include programs about Mariupol and Russia, aim to focus on investigative projects that cover stories from regions where reporting is challenging.

The newly formed company launches with two projects broadcast on the same night: the BBC project *Iran: The People’s Story* and Channel 4's *The Taliban’s War on Women*. Both films examine authoritarian governance and required clandestine production methods.

## Navigating Industry Pressures And Funding

According to the founders, the decision to start an independent company stemmed from shrinking market budgets and financial pressures within the current affairs television sector. Securing complete funding from a single public service broadcaster for complex international documentaries has become increasingly difficult, prompting the filmmakers to manage their own capital and production control.

Sisu Pictures partnered with broadcasters including France-German network Arte and an undisclosed American entity for its initial releases. The leadership stated they are developing further confidential projects while maintaining strict verification standards and editorial impartiality.

## Focus On Facts And Impartiality

Addressing the broader media landscape, the co-founders emphasized an adherence to verified reporting amidst rising pressures for journalists to engage on social media. They highlighted the distinction between impartial journalism and advocacy, noting that transparency regarding perspectives and contributors remains essential for documentary makers.
