## New guest stars join the medical drama

"Grey’s Anatomy" is adding three guest stars to its upcoming Season 23 cast: Sarah Levy, Martin Martinez and Trevor Tordjman.

Sarah Levy has been cast in the role of Corrie. According to the official character description, Corrie is described as witty and a bit of an oddball who is very pregnant, carrying significant anxiety about giving birth to her second child following a traumatic experience with her first child. Levy is widely known for her role as Twyla Sands in “Schitt’s Creek,” and her other acting credits include “SurrealEstate” and the film “The Monkey.”

## Character details for Martinez and Tordjman

Martin Martinez joins the series to play Charlie. The character has experienced a rough upbringing and trauma, resulting in a tough exterior and struggles with trusting others. Martinez's previous television credits include appearances in “Never Have I Ever,” “Magnum P.I.” and “Primo.”

Trevor Tordjman takes on the role of Thomas, described as a fun-loving and charming boy next door who is unaware of how cute he is. Thomas arrives at the hospital to help ensure that his brother’s girlfriend is okay. Tordjman's prior work includes roles in the Disney titles “Zombies” and “Bunk’d,” as well as “The Next Step,” “The Studio” and “The Sex Lives of College Girls.”

The casting updates arrive following a major cast shakeup during the Season 22 finale, which featured the departures of characters Owen Hunt and Teddy Altman, portrayed by Kevin McKidd and Kim Raver.
