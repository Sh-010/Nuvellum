## Overcoming a difficult 2026 campaign

For George Russell, victory at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix served as vindication, providing proof that he had moved past a challenging period during the 2026 Formula 1 season. The Mercedes driver held off Red Bull's Max Verstappen in a close finish to claim his first win since the end of June, marking his third victory of the year.

Russell began the season as a championship favorite, but teammate Kimi Antonelli secured three wins during Russell's seven-race winless streak, bringing Antonelli's season total to eight victories. Antonelli adapted quickly to the new Mercedes following major rule changes, while Russell struggled to find comfort in a car that did not initially suit his driving style.

Reflecting on the struggles, Mercedes deputy team principal Bradley Lord noted that Russell experienced difficult moments and soul-searching before the summer break. Through hard work and analysis alongside his engineers, Russell worked to adapt his driving style, leading to improved confidence and performance following the break.

In Baku, Russell delivered what he described as the most dominant weekend of his career, leading from start to finish despite two mid-race safety cars that brought Verstappen onto his tail. The victory, combined with Antonelli finishing fifth after a mistake in qualifying that forced him to start 16th, reduced the points gap between the two teammates to 66 points with seven or eight races remaining.

Antonelli described his weekend as his worst performance-based outing of the year, admitting that his mindset and confidence levels were affected following his qualifying error. Despite the setback, Antonelli maintained a significant lead in the standings.

Russell stated that he plans to focus on his own performance rather than the championship standings, noting that less pressure rests on him compared to his teammate. Mercedes anticipates an upcoming upgrade package ahead of the races in Bahrain and Malaysia as the team looks toward the remainder of the season.
