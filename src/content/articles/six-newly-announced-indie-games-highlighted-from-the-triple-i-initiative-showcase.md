The Triple-i Initiative showcase recently featured 40 independent games during a 45-minute stream. Alongside updates for previously anticipated titles such as *The Talos Principle 3* and *Pony Island 2: Panda Circus*, the event highlighted an array of newly announced indie projects planned for Steam.

## Puzzle adventures and horror titles

Among the featured games is developer Amanita Design's ***Machinarium 2***, a sequel to the 2009 puzzle adventure cult classic *Machinarium*, which continues to feature hand-drawn art. In the psychological horror genre, developer 12AM Blackout introduced ***Jiaohun***, a surreal debut described as a trip through a unique version of hell. Meanwhile, Critical Reflex is publishing ***Militsioner***, an immersive sim from Mouthwashing publisher that tasks players with evading a giant police officer looming over a city.

## Card battling and strategy projects

Other revealed titles explore different gameplay mechanics and historical settings:

* **Lazare’s Sacrifice:** Developed in collaboration with Gwent creator Damien Monnier, this card-battling game serves as a retelling of the French Revolution.
* **Prison of Husks:** Created by developer Glass Head Dolls, this upcoming Soulslike employs fuzzy polygons to mimic the visual style of a FromSoftware game running on a PlayStation 2.
* **Gods With Badges:** Developer Strange Scaffold follows up on its previous tactics work with a turn-based strategy game focusing on themes of policing and responsibility.
