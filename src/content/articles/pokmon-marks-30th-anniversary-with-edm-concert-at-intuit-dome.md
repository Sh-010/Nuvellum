## Celebrating Three Decades with Electronic Dance Music

The Pokémon franchise marked its 30th anniversary with a large-scale electronic dance music event titled Pokémon Night Out at the Intuit Dome in Los Angeles. The production combined creatures from the trading card and video game series with laser displays, pyrotechnics, and visual effects.

The collaboration between the family-friendly franchise and the techno genre was described by brand representatives as a natural alignment of values centered around community and connection. While the brand has existed for three decades, organizers noted that a concert of this scale is a first for the company.

## Performances and Visuals

DJs Marshmello and Alison Wonderland performed custom sets accompanied by massive video walls displaying animated creatures. Alison Wonderland opened the evening with a countdown of various characters, a remix of the television series theme song, and an appearance by a neon-clad Pikachu mascot on stage.

Marshmello followed with a set featuring floating digital booths, synchronized lasers, and animated vignettes depicting battles between legendary characters such as Groudon and Kyogre. The event was livestreamed for remote audiences, and an additional performance is scheduled for the O2 Arena in London.

Attendees filled the venue, with many participating in cosplay dressed as trainers and characters from the series as the franchise looks toward its future events.
