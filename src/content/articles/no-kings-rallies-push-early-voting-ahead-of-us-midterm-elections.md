Organizers of the "No Kings" protests held demonstrations across the United States to encourage Americans to cast early ballots ahead of the November 3 midterm elections. 

Saturday's event, titled "No Kings: Vote Early," marked the fourth protest since President Donald Trump returned to office. Organizers described the day of action as a nonpartisan effort to bring communities together, help voters make a plan, and ensure eligible voters understand their options. 

With more than 3,000 events planned across the country, the demonstrations focused on turnout as Republicans face difficult races to maintain their majority in Congress. Opinion polls indicate that Democrats are positioned to potentially regain control of the House of Representatives, while control of the Senate remains undecided.

## Trump's stance on mail-in voting

President Trump has frequently criticized mail-in voting and attempted to limit the practice before the midterms, though he routinely votes by mail himself, citing his job as an exception. He has attributed his 2020 election defeat to election fraud without offering evidence. 

Surveys indicate that limiting mail-in ballots could favor Republicans because Democratic voters rely more heavily on the system. Meanwhile, critics have expressed concern that the Trump administration might attempt to interfere with midterm outcomes if Republicans lose their congressional majority.
