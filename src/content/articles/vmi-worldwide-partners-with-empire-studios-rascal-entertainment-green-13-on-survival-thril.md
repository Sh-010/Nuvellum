## Production partnership and filming details

Empire Studios, Rascal Entertainment, Green 13 Films, and VMI Worldwide have formed a partnership for the survival thriller *Snow Predator*. The project is currently in production at Shinfield Studios in the United Kingdom.

The feature is directed by Coz Greenop and written by Charley McDougall. According to production details, the narrative follows a young thrill-seeker named Betty—played by Eloise Lovell Anderson—who inherits a remote cabin from her estranged father, Andy, portrayed by Jason Flemyng. Betty ventures into the frozen Canadian wilderness with friends, where they become trapped by a brutal storm and hunted by a polar bear. This development triggers a race against time for a Search and Rescue team led by Scott, played by Daniel Stisen, to reach the group.

## Cast and crew

The ensemble cast for *Snow Predator* also includes Genevieve Chenneour, Jack McEvoy, Daniel O’Reilly, Jack Parr, Cole Anderson James, Crystal Wright, Melina Farahani, Neve O’Reilly, Mya Greenop, and Tim Cullingworth-Hudson.

The film is produced by Charley McDougall, Jamie McLeod-Ross, Luke Adams, Andre Relis, and Jessica Russo. Executive producers include Daniel O’Reilly, Jade Jelly, Julie Paquit, Mickey Guerin, Bradley Stryker, and Rustin Matthew, while Becca Hirani of EMRJ Entertainment acts as production manager.

Speaking about the project, writer Charley McDougall stated that the ambition of the production is to deliver a visceral, edge-of-your-seat experience associated with the genre. VMI Worldwide is managing worldwide sales for the film.
