At the Beverly Wilshire for the Simon Wiesenthal Center's annual gala, Skydance co-CEO Ynon Kreiz received the organization's 2026 Humanitarian Award. Presented by David Ellison, Gal Gadot, and Jeffrey Katzenberg, the executive used his acceptance speech to address global hostility toward Jewish communities.

## Combating Hatred and Corporate Responsibility

Kreiz told the packed ballroom that ancient hatred has found new platforms to spread. He stated that business leadership comes with moral responsibility, adding that defending Israel does not mean the nation is beyond criticism. 

Earlier in the evening, Ellison praised Kreiz as a leader who embodies courage and conviction. Video remarks from Israeli President Isaac Herzog were also broadcast to the crowd.

## Star-Studded Gathering in Beverly Hills

The gala drew numerous prominent figures from the entertainment industry, including Vin Diesel, Jerry Bruckheimer, Casey Bloys, Lior Raz, and Lawrence Bender. Former CNN anchor Brianna Golodryga received the Jewish Heritage Award during the ceremony, while Gadot spoke about the honoree's character and passion.

Event chairs included Shari Redstone, along with Cheryl and Haim Saban. The gathering highlighted industry leaders addressing contemporary social issues while reflecting Hollywood's shifting corporate landscape following recent studio mergers.
