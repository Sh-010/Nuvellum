Following the completion of a major corporate merger earlier in October, regional leadership has addressed the ongoing transition within the media sector. Christina Sulebakk, who oversees operations in the Nordics, Germany, and Benelux, spoke publicly about the process during the Munich Media Days conference.

## Managing industry-wide transformation

Sulebakk, a longtime veteran of the organization and its predecessors, noted that the current transition carries unique weight because the broader media ecosystem is undergoing simultaneous shifts. While acknowledging that staff members often experience mixed reactions to such reorganizations—with some viewing change as an opportunity and others expressing concern over job security and leadership structures—she emphasized the importance of active management.

“We’re doing our best to manage the change, but I’m not naïve,” Sulebakk stated, pointing to parallel shifts at other major media entities.

## Future strategy and partnerships

Addressing her role within the newly formed $110 billion entity, Sulebakk indicated an intent to pursue active market opportunities rather than maintaining a purely symbolic position. Her strategy involves exploring potential collaborations with digital platforms such as YouTube alongside traditional windowing options with streaming services including Netflix, Prime Video, and Hulu. 

While international operations are largely directed from Los Angeles by JB Perrette, the final organizational structure for non-U.S. territories and territorial divisions remains under review.
