## Return to HBO

HBO has greenlit a revival of the comedy-drama series \"Girls,\" created by Lena Dunham. The network has ordered a limited, five-episode run, with production scheduled to begin in 2027. The revival comes a decade after the conclusion of the series, which originally ran on HBO from 2012 to 2017 across six seasons.

All four core cast members are set to reprise their original roles: Lena Dunham as Hannah Horvath, Alison Williams as Marnie Michaels, Jemima Kirke as Jessa Johansson, and Zosia Mamet as Shoshanna Shapiro. In addition, Andrew Rannells will return to play Elijah Krantz.

## Production and Creative Team

Judd Apatow is returning as an executive producer representing Apatow Productions, alongside Dunham, Ilene S. Landress, and Michael P. Cohen under Dunham’s Good Thing Going banner. Williams, Kirke, Mamet, and Rannells will serve as co-executive producers on the new episodes.

Commenting on the decision to revive the show, Dunham stated that she and the team only wanted to return if there was a specific story to tell. She noted that the idea came to her around her fortieth birthday as she considered the characters in the present day.

Amy Gravitt, executive vice president and head of HBO & HBO Max comedy programming, stated that the network is eager to see the characters' progression and how they have grown since the original run.

## Notable Absences and Past Revelations

Two significant figures from the original series' run were absent from HBO's revival announcement: actor Adam Driver, who played Hannah's on-and-off partner Adam, and co-showrunner Jenni Konner.

In her memoir \"Famesick,\" released earlier in the year, Dunham detailed negative experiences involving both individuals. Her accounts included allegations regarding Driver's conduct on set during filming, as well as descriptions of a strained professional and personal relationship with Konner over time.
