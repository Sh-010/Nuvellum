Voting for the Golden Globes is underway, setting off a competitive race featuring hundreds of submissions across various film and acting categories.

## Breakdown of film and acting categories

A total of 170 distinct films have been submitted for consideration. In the major picture categories, 88 films are competing for best motion picture in the drama field, while 43 entries target the musical or comedy category. Notable projects in the running include Christopher Nolan’s "The Odyssey" and Denis Villeneuve’s "Dune: Part Three" for drama, alongside Destin Daniel Cretton’s "Spider-Man: Brand New Day" and David Fincher’s "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth" for musical or comedy.

The six acting categories account for 475 different performers. The leading drama actor race features 69 submissions representing 64 performers, including Brad Pitt and Robert Pattinson. Meanwhile, the supporting actor category stands out as the most crowded field, boasting 221 submissions from 198 performers. Mark Ruffalo leads multiple entries in that category with three different projects.

## Directors, screenplays and international features

Behind the camera, 131 filmmakers are attached to 123 films competing for the best director race, featuring established names like Steven Spielberg and Christopher Nolan. The screenplay category has gathered 121 submissions, including Quentin Tarantino's script for "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth."

In additional categories, the original score race features 100 entries with composers John Williams and Hans Zimmer in contention, while the original song category counts 51 submissions. The animated feature film section includes 24 eligible titles, and the non-English language film category contains 42 submitted projects representing various international regions, including entries from Japan and South Korea.
