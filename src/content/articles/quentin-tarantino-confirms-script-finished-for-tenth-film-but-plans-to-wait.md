Director Quentin Tarantino has confirmed that he has written a script for his tenth and final feature film, but announced he will not pursue production immediately. 

## Plans for the final film

Tarantino shared the update during an appearance on "The Graham Norton Show," marking his first time on the program in over a decade. He appeared alongside guests Ellen DeGeneres, Colin Farrell, and Gemma Chan. 

Discussing his retirement plan following his tenth feature, Tarantino said he wrote a script he really likes. However, he stated he intends to finish his theatrical play, see who he is, spend time with his family, and live a little before deciding his next steps on the film project. 

## Focus on "The Popinjay Cavalier"

Before returning to film work, Tarantino is turning his attention to his play, "The Popinjay Cavalier." He revealed that the project initially began as a screenplay before he realized it worked better as a stage production.

Starring Sacha Baron Cohen, the swashbuckling comedy is scheduled to open at London’s Phoenix Theatre next March, following previews in late February. The production is slated for a three-month run, closing on June 13. 

Tarantino's most recent feature film release was 2019's "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood," which received multiple Academy Award nominations and wins.
