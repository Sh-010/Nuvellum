## Addressing leadership changes and viewing trends

Netflix co-CEOs Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters spoke with Wall Street analysts following the company's third-quarter earnings release, addressing a domestic slowdown in viewing and ad sales that prompted a major content leadership restructuring. The executive shift included the departure of chief content officer Bela Bajaria and film chief Dan Lin, with former Fox and Disney executive Peter Rice appointed to succeed Bajaria.

Despite softening metrics in the United States, Peters assured analysts that the platform's core operational fundamentals remain solid. He highlighted that subscriber retention surpassed expectations domestically and internationally, even following the implementation of subscription price increases.

## International growth and content strategy

While the domestic market experienced a soft period, Peters noted that operations outside the United States delivered robust engagement and viewing figures, fueled by local programming. Specifically, view hours in India increased by 24% on an absolute basis during the third quarter.

Sarandos emphasized that the company is prioritizing execution to produce standout hits that can travel globally. Discussing the appointment of Rice, Sarandos stated that the incoming executive possesses a blend of artistic and commercial expertise tailored for the platform's upcoming phase.

Chief financial officer Spencer Neumann added that the streaming service maintains a stable growth model that avoids over-reliance on any single genre, format, or geographic region.
