Japanese director Ishikawa Hiroshi is returning to feature filmmaking after a 13-year break with his latest project, "The Blue We Touch." Known for previous works such as "Tokyo.sora," "Su-ki-da.," and "Petal Dance," the filmmaker served as writer, director, and editor for the new ensemble drama.

## Cast and storyline

The narrative centers on complex interpersonal relationships and reunites former acquaintances. Nao stars as Mitsu, a 29-year-old contract web designer seeking steady employment and meaningful audience engagement. Her character develops a connection with Fujino, played by Nakajima Ayumu, who manages human resources at her workplace.

The cast also features Karata Erika as Ayane, a night worker studying to become a public prosecutor, and Aoki Yuzu as Tomoki, an art student struggling with figure drawing. Additional cast members include Nakano Arisa, who previously appeared in Wim Wenders' "Perfect Days."

## Production and distribution

Currently in post-production, the feature has a scheduled Japanese release for 2027. Distributor Bitters End introduced the project prior to the Tokyo International Film Festival's TIFFCOM market. 

Ishikawa transitioned from directing commercials to feature films with his 2002 debut, "Tokyo.sora." His cinematic style is noted for its atmospheric depiction of everyday life and deliberate use of color.
