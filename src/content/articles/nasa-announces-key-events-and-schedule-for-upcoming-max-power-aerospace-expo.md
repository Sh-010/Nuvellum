NASA has outlined its schedule of activities for the upcoming MAX POWER aerospace exhibition and air show. The event is scheduled to run from Friday, November 6, through Sunday, November 8, primarily hosted at and near the Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

## Exhibits and Air Demonstrations

The expo will feature park experiences highlighting new aircraft, autonomous vehicles, and emerging technologies from the agency and external partners. NASA plans to exhibit its quiet supersonic X-59 plane, while collaborators will present spacecraft and Moon rovers. The exhibition also marks the conclusion of the agency's recent Inspiration Tour, which featured multiple flyovers.

Public events open at 9 a.m. EST on Saturday and Sunday, providing visitors opportunities to interact with leadership, astronauts, engineers, and scientists. Scheduled flight demonstrations include multiple flyovers by an F-5 and a MiG-29 featuring agency leadership.

## Media Briefings and Lunar Updates

NASA plans to host several media briefings and panel discussions throughout the weekend. On Saturday, November 7, the Artemis III crew is scheduled to appear on stage, followed by a media availability. Later that day, agency officials will hold a press conference at 5 p.m. to deliver an update on the lunar South Pole Moon Base, designated as humanity's first long-term lunar exploration outpost.

Additional scheduled activities include a nuclear media gaggle, presentations on the Roman Space Telescope, a student challenge announcement, and an appearance by the SpaceX Crew-12 astronauts on Sunday, November 8.
