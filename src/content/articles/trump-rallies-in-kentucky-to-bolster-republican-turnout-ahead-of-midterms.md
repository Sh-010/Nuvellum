## Rally in Kentucky

President Donald Trump held a rally on Saturday in Highland Heights, Kentucky, to support Republican candidates including Ed Gallrein, who is running for the U.S. House. Trump spoke to bolster Republican turnout amid midterm struggles for his party, telling attendees, “I can do stuff that is unbelievable.”

During his remarks, Trump discussed his use of executive power, pointing to his decisions to alter geographic references, such as renaming the Gulf of Mexico to the Gulf of America and referring to Lake Ontario as Lake America. “I don’t think I could have done this in my first term. This is a much more powerful term,” he said.

## Promises and political context

Trump encouraged voters to approach the election by asking them to treat his name as if it were on the ballot, as control of both chambers of Congress remains contested. Amid voter frustrations over tariffs, gas prices, and an ongoing conflict with Iran, Trump asserted that the war would end soon and predicted gas prices would drop.

He also made several electoral promises, telling the crowd that a Republican victory in the House and Senate would result in a $5,000 payment for every adult American. Additionally, Trump made a conditional promise that the Department of Defense would invest in revitalizing a drug manufacturing facility in Kentucky if Republicans win.

## Congressional primary context

Trump brought Gallrein onto the stage during the event. Gallrein won the Republican primary after defeating Representative Thomas Massie. Trump criticized Massie during the speech following Massie's opposition to the Iran war and his push to release Jeffrey Epstein files, while promoting Gallrein as a loyal replacement. The crowd's response to the remarks about Massie was mixed, with part of the arena remaining silent.
