## Weekend box office leaders

Constantin Film and Sony Pictures' horror reboot “Resident Evil” captured the top spot at the mainland China box office for the weekend of Oct. 9–11, according to data from Artisan Gateway. The video game adaptation collected RMB81.2 million ($12.1 million) over the three-day frame, bringing its cumulative total to $40.7 million following its Oct. 5 rollout during the latter stretch of the Golden Week holiday.

New Classics Pictures’ crime procedural drama “Traces of Justice” slipped to second place in its sophomore session, earning $8.3 million. Directed by Chen Sicheng and co-written with Zhang Ji and Yan Zijie, the period investigation drama has accumulated $73.4 million since opening on Oct. 1. The multi-decade narrative, based on real events, stars Zhang Yi, Ma Li, Chen Minghao, Karry Wang, Fan Chengcheng, and Chen Feiyu.

## Mid-tier releases and holdovers

Beijing Joy Fun Pictures’ family body-swap comedy “Mr. & Mrs. Pardon” secured third position with $5.7 million over the weekend, pushing its cumulative gross to $43.6 million. Directed by Shen Chunyang and written by Ma Duo, the film stars Shen Chunyang alongside Xiao Shenyang, Jia Bing, and Yu Yang.

CMC Pictures’ revenge thriller sequel “Go for Broke 2” placed fourth, adding $1 million in its second weekend for a cumulative total of $7.9 million since its Oct. 3 release. Written and directed by Marc Ma, the action feature stars Isabella Leong, Wen Junhui, and Sammo Hung.

Rounding out the top five, Dirty Monkeys’ war comedy-drama “Once Upon a Time in the Middle East” took in $800,000 in its ninth weekend. China’s entry to the Oscars’ international feature category, directed by Wen Muye and starring Shen Teng, Jiang Qiming, Aarif Rahman, and Omar Sherif, has reached a $353 million cume since opening on Aug. 11.

## Overall market performance

Mainland China’s overall weekend box office fell to $30.8 million as theatrical traffic normalized following the National Day holiday rush. According to Artisan Gateway figures, year-to-date box office revenue stands at $4.63 billion, marking a 29.4% decline compared to the same period in 2025.
