## Statement from Kimberly Hefner

Kimberly Hefner, who was married to Hugh Hefner from 1989 until their divorce was finalized in 2010, published a social media statement defending her late husband. Her remarks followed statements from Crystal Harris, another former wife of the Playboy magazine founder, who urged actor Jessica Biel to reconsider playing a fictional version of Hefner's wife in the upcoming film *Playmates*.

Harris previously alleged she experienced sexual, emotional, financial, and physical abuse during her marriage. She detailed her experiences in her 2024 memoir, *Only Say Good Things: Surviving Playboy and Finding Myself*.

In her post, Kimberly Hefner stated she was married to Hefner for over 21 years and shared a loving bond that continued until his death on September 27, 2017. "I often told him if you look up the word gentleman in the dictionary you would find his name and photograph," she wrote, describing him as kind, loving, generous, and philanthropic.

## Background on the Marriages and Film

Before marrying Hugh Hefner, Kimberly met him while modeling as a Playmate of the Month centerfold. Crystal Harris met him at a Halloween party at the Playboy Mansion when she was a 21-year-old college student, later moving into the mansion and marrying him in 2012 until his death in 2017.

The upcoming Searchlight film *Playmates* revolves around Elle, a 13-year-old who considers the Playboy Mansion her second home, and Anna, a hopeful vying to be Playmate of the Month, as they form a friendship. The project is written and directed by Lorraine Nicholson, with Bill Pullman cast as Hugh Hefner and Jessica Biel attached to play a fictional version of his wife.
