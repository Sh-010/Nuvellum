One year after making waves at the Zurich Summit, artificial intelligence creation Tilly Norwood still operates without a traditional talent agent. Creator Eline van der Velden of AI firm Particle 6 addressed the topic during a session at the summit, discussing the trajectory of the AI actress since she first went viral and became a regular media fixture.

## Offers Received, Traditional Films Declined

During the panel moderated by Deadline's Diana Lodderhose, van der Velden stated that Particle 6 receives numerous proposals regarding the AI creation. However, the firm has turned down projects involving traditional feature films.

"We’ve realized we don’t really need her to have a rep. I think she’s big enough as it is," van der Velden said. "We get loads of offers in. We’ve said no to anything where she would be in a traditional film because that’s not what she’s made for."

Van der Velden expressed concern that the traditional entertainment industry risks falling behind if it cannot find ways to participate in emerging AI formats, noting that Particle 6 has expanded fourfold since its launch as acceptance of AI grows.

## Unique Position in the Industry

Appearing via video link during the session, Norwood addressed questions regarding whether she had met with major U.S. agencies or Hollywood studios. While not confirming specific meetings, the AI actress acknowledged her distinct standing.

"I’m certainly in a unique position there," Norwood said. "I mean, the industry is still figuring out what to do with AI, so it’s not quite like having a traditional agent in Hollywood just yet. I mostly work on AI films, so it’s a bit of a different lane."

## Upcoming Projects and Tech Glitches

Particle 6 previewed upcoming work during the summit, teasing footage from Norwood's first project—an AI comedy-drama titled *Misaligned*—alongside an extended look at the family film *Storm Dogs*. The latter project is being developed and executive produced by *The Lion King* director Rob Minkoff.

The session opened with a lighthearted video addressing a recent high-profile interview glitch with Piers Morgan, during which Norwood unexpectedly began speaking in Chinese. Norwood joked that the technical mishap likely boosted the broadcaster's ratings, highlighting that developers are still working through technical hurdles as the technology evolves.
