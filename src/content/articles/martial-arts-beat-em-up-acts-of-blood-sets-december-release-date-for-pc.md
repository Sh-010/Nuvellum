Developer Eksil Team has announced that the martial arts beat-'em-up game *Acts of Blood* will launch for Windows PC on Dec. 4. While the title was previously announced exclusively for PC, the studio also confirmed that console versions for the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X are scheduled to arrive sometime in 2027.

## Gameplay and inspirations

*Acts of Blood* casts players as Hendra, a protagonist on a quest for revenge that takes place in the fictional streets of Bandapa, Indonesia, as he investigates the group responsible for his family's murder. Developer Fajrul FN, who initially began the project as a solo endeavor before forming Eksil Team, stated that the game draws inspiration from action films like *The Raid*. 

The combat system combines counter-based mechanics reminiscent of *Sleeping Dogs* with layered combos similar to *Sifu*. Players can utilize melee weapons, pick up firearms, and interact with the environment, alongside violent combat features including brutal takedowns, dismemberment, and X-ray finishers.

## Demo reception

Ahead of the December launch, a playable demo is available on Steam. The trial version has garnered over 1,500 player reviews categorized as Very Positive, with users praising the combat's weight and animations.
