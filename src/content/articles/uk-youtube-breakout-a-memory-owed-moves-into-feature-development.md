The British short film *A Memory Owed* is advancing into feature development following its release on YouTube late last year. Written and directed by Martin Hemingway-Moseley, the project has accumulated more than 600,000 views and a 99% approval rating on the platform.

## Plot and cast

The original short film follows a World War II veteran visiting an RAF museum in London, featuring flashbacks to his wartime service alongside a parallel story about his young carer who is struggling financially. The production stars BAFTA nominee James Bolam and actress Rumi Sutton.

Stephen Fry described the project as "impressive and moving," and producer Zoe Lewin oversaw the short's release. 

## Moving to the big screen

Following its online reception, the creative team is working on a treatment and preparing to approach potential partners for the expanded version. The short has already drawn interest from at least one distributor, an unsolicited donation for early development, and requests for screenings from educators, community groups, and veterans' organisations including the RAF Benevolent Fund.

While the short was filmed at the Lincolnshire Aviation Heritage Centre, Hemingway-Moseley stated that the planned feature is intended as "a love letter to Bomber County as a whole," adding that the team envisions the longer adaptation incorporating a road trip element.
