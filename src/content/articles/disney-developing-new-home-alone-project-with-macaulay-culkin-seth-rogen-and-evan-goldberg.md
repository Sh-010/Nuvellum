## Early development at Disney

A fresh iteration of the classic holiday comedy "Home Alone" is currently in early development at Disney. The original star of the 1990 festive hit, Macaulay Culkin, is teaming up with Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg to shape the screenplay. Rogen and Goldberg will also serve as producers through their Point Grey production banner.

Specific plot details and additional casting information have not yet been disclosed. When contacted regarding the project, the studio declined to provide a statement.

## History of the franchise

The original motion picture debuted in 1990, featuring a young Culkin as Kevin McCallister. In the story, the character is accidentally left behind while his family departs for a Christmas vacation, ultimately forcing him to defend his home from a pair of burglars.

A successful 1992 sequel, "Home Alone 2: Lost in New York," repeated the premise in an urban setting. Several subsequent installments followed over the decades, including a 1997 reboot and three additional films, though the upcoming project marks a return for the franchise's original leading actor.
