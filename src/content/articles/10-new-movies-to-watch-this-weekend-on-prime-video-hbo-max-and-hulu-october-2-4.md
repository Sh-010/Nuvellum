## Streaming and VOD film releases for October 2-4

A selection of new movies and streaming titles are available across various platforms for the weekend of October 2-4, ranging from horror and animation to historical drama and mountain-climbing documentaries.

## Horror, comedy, and animation highlights

* **Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma** (Mubi): Directed by Jane Schoenbrun and starring Hannah Einbinder and Gillian Anderson, the horror film follows an indie director rebooting a slasher franchise.
* **Coyote vs. ACME** (VOD): Directed by Dave Green and starring Lana Condor and Will Forte, this live-action and animated hybrid comedy follows Wile E. Coyote suing the ACME Corporation.
* **Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle** (Hulu): Directed by Haruo Sotozaki, this animated sequel follows the Demon Slayer Corps hunting Muzan through a pocket dimension.
* **Soulm8te** (Hulu): Directed by Kate Dolan and starring Lily Sullivan and David Rysdahl, this sci-fi thriller is set in the world of M3gan and follows a grieving engineer designing an AI lovebot.
* **Evil Dead Burn** (HBO Max): Directed by Sébastien Vaniček and starring Souheila Yacoub, this horror film follows a grieving woman hunted by Deadites.
* **Lockbox** (MGM+): Directed by Daniel Stamm and starring Carla Gugino, the horror film follows a woman moving to a quiet town after her mother's death.
* **Infirmary** (Shudder): Directed by Nicholas Pineda, this horror film follows a rookie security guard experiencing supernatural occurrences at an abandoned psychiatric hospital.

## Dramas and documentaries

* **The Uprising** (VOD): Directed by Paul Greengrass and starring Andrew Garfield and Jamie Bell, this historical drama is inspired by the Peasants' Revolt of 1381 under King Richard II.
* **Union County** (VOD): Directed by Adam Meeks and starring Will Poulter, this drama is set amid the US opioid crisis and follows an addict navigating a court-mandated recovery program.
* **The Last First: Winter K2** (Apple TV): Directed by Amir Bar-Lev, this documentary follows the 2020–2021 winter ascent of K2, which led to the deaths of five people.
