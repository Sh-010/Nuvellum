Although Larian Studios has explicitly stated that it will not produce official expansions or a *Baldur's Gate 4*, the game's community continues to generate substantial custom content. At a 2024 GDC panel, founder Swen Vincke confirmed that the studio intended to move away from D&D and start a new project. Despite this closure on official add-ons, multiple custom campaigns have launched recently, with several more currently in development.

## Unlocking developer tools

Larian's official mod support was not originally designed to accommodate full custom-campaign creation. However, modders have bypassed these limitations by using unlocked toolkits like Moonglasses, which removes standard restrictions built into Larian's shipped editor. While the official editor disables features such as building new levels, writing new dialogue, or designing combat encounters, third-party tools grant access to developer mode, allowing creators to build entirely new stories from scratch.

Community initiatives have also driven releases. Projects like *A Dream of Silence: ReVamped* were designed specifically for community events such as the BG3 July Modathon, demonstrating that the synchronized release windows are often intentional.

## A growing library of custom campaigns

Recent fan-made additions offer varied gameplay styles:

* **A Dream of Silence: ReVamped:** Adapts interactive fiction into a six-hour, four-ending story set in a shifting nightmare version of Cazador’s mansion.
* **The Sunless Citadel:** Focuses on a linear dungeon crawl, prioritizing combat and exploration over branching narratives.
* **Act Two Expansion:** Expands the second section of the game into the Chionthar Wilds, adding over 70 items and 30 new fights.
* **Dinner With Strahd:** Features a self-contained encounter with voice acting for a D&D villain.
* **Path to Menzoberranzan:** An upcoming three-act campaign featuring its own companions and a new protagonist set a century after *Baldur's Gate 2*.

## Technical limitations and compatibility

Unlike official studio releases, these fan-made expansions are not currently available on consoles and require a PC installation through Nexus Mods. Some projects remain in alpha or beta phases and lack features like camp systems.

Additionally, combining certain mods can cause technical conflicts. For instance, running *A Dream of Silence: ReVamped* alongside *Dinner With Strahd* can trigger visual glitches that swap out elements of Cazador's appearance. Despite these limitations, the active modding scene continues to provide new content for players replaying the game.
