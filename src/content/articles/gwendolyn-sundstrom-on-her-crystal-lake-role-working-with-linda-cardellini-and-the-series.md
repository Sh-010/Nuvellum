Actor Gwendolyn Sundstrom has shared insights into her role as Grace, the childhood friend of Jason Voorhees, in the Peacock slasher prequel series *Crystal Lake*. Filming the Brad Caleb Kane series in New Jersey when she was 13 years old, Sundstrom described the character as her favorite to play so far.

## Building Grace and working with Linda Cardellini

The series explores the accidental drowning of young Jason, depicted in the opening scene of the first episode where Grace throws a bully's ball into the lake. Flashbacks continue to follow the adolescent friendship between Jason (Callum Vinson) and Grace, an underdog character who struggles to mourn his death.

Sundstrom noted that she and Vinson shared good real-life chemistry. Meanwhile, Linda Cardellini portrays Pam Voorhees, who takes an interest in Grace as she deals with an abusive and neglectful mother. Sundstrom praised working with Cardellini, stating that she learned a lot from her while navigating the complex maternal dynamic between their characters.

## The killer twist

Later in the series, Grace encounters a pack of traveling hippies and experiences a drug trip that recontextualizes the franchise's very first kill. The sequence reveals that Grace, rather than Pam, was the person who killed counselors Claudette and Barry in the first-person POV sequence.

Sundstrom stated that she enjoyed filming the dramatic and gritty scene. Series creator Brad Caleb Kane explained that the retcon was intended to surprise the audience and provide a fresh perspective for viewers familiar with the original 1980 film.

*Crystal Lake* is currently streaming on Peacock.
