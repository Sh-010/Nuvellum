Actors John Malkovich and Rosalind Eleazar are set to headline a new play titled "History" written by Rebecca Reid. The production will run at London's Wyndham's Theatre from May 17 through August 7, 2027.

## Plot and Creative Team

The narrative centers on a character named Wren, whose creative momentum stalled following the birth of her child. Seeking a reckless change, Wren invites her former partner James to lunch. James previously defined her life, and the encounter reveals that a relationship she believed finished is still active.

Matthew Dunster will direct the limited engagement, marking Reid's debut as a playwright on the West End. The production is produced by Fiery Angel and Kater Gordon, with additional casting announcements expected later.

## Background of the Cast and Crew

Malkovich expressed enthusiasm about participating in the project alongside Eleazar and Dunster. Eleazar noted that collaborating with Malkovich has been a long-held ambition.

Reid described the work as a humorous, provocative examination of marriage, power dynamics, motherhood, and impulsive decisions. Dunster praised the incoming headliners, noting Malkovich's established stature and Eleazar's dynamic presence across stage and screen performances.
