## Nationwide security measures and route changes

France prepared for another wave of cost-of-living demonstrations as authorities deployed extra police forces across the country. The heightened security posture aims to prevent a recurrence of the violence that erupted following previous weekend demonstrations. In the capital, a protest march got underway at Porte d'Italie following a late alteration to the planned itinerary.

The demonstrations originated from student-led protests highlighting inadequate school conditions and insufficient resources. Government statistics estimated that more than half a million individuals participated in the prior weekend's rallies, and authorities anticipated similarly substantial crowds.

## Closures in Paris and police actions in Lyon

Cities throughout the country heightened precautionary measures in response to the unrest. In Paris, prominent cultural institutions including the Louvre, the Orsay museum, and the Picasso museum suspended operations. Additionally, numerous luxury retail stores opted to close and board up storefront windows following instances of looting during the preceding weekend.

Meanwhile, law enforcement in Lyon intervened against a banned demonstration. Police deployed tear gas to disperse a crowd of approximately 150 individuals who had assembled to protest.
