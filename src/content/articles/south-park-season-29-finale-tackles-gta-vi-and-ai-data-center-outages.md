## Season 29 finale addresses gaming and tech issues

The animated comedy *South Park* concluded its 29th season with an episode titled "City Endings," which combined the long-awaited release of the video game *Grand Theft Auto VI* with local infrastructure disruptions. During the finale, the fictional town faced severe connectivity problems caused by artificial intelligence data centers operated by Billionaire Weenietown. These facilities were established in the area after Mayor McDaniels agreed to host them.

As power failures disrupted the community, the mayor deflected blame away from the facility, falsely suggesting that foreign cyber attacks were responsible. This misinformation led local residents to target Mr. Kim’s City Parlor under false pretenses.

## Supernatural encounters and character returns

Amidst the network disruptions, main characters Kyle, Stan, and Cartman encountered the ghost of Kenny, who had died earlier in the season following an e-bike explosion involving a strapped bomb. Investigating the connectivity issues led the group to Butters, who remained unaffected by the outages after receiving financial perks and a gaming setup from Billionaire Weenietown.

During gameplay, a digital version of Kenny emerged from the screen to relay a warning to his peers. The narrative concluded as the children confronted local authorities over their cooperation with the incoming billionaires.

## Resolution and topical satire

Mayor McDaniels eventually acknowledged responsibility for the billionaires' impact on the town. To appease the frustrated residents, she had Mr. Kim launched into space aboard a rocket named WeenieX. The storyline wrapped up a season-long arc touching on corporate influence, technology shifts, and contemporary cultural trends.
