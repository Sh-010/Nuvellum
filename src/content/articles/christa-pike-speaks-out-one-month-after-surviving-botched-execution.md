## Statement released after failed execution

Christa Pike has marked one month since surviving a failed execution by the state of Tennessee, releasing a statement through her legal team that laments the incompetence leading to the ordeal. Pike was strapped to a gurney in the execution chamber on 30 September, facing the death penalty for a 1995 murder committed when she was 18.

Execution personnel spent nearly an hour attempting to establish an intravenous line to administer a massive dose of the sedative pentobarbital. The procedure failed, and Pike survived. In her statement, she described enduring continuous physical suffering and mental anguish since being injected.

## Confinement and recovery

Pike is currently recovering in the infirmary at the Debra Johnson rehabilitation center in Nashville following 10 days of intensive hospital treatment. According to her statement, she is largely held in solitary confinement, mirroring the isolation she experienced for her first 28 years on death row.

The failed procedure has prompted the Republican governor of Tennessee, Bill Lee, to order a pause on further executions pending an outside review. Governor Lee stated he expects Pike's death sentence to remain unchanged unless directed otherwise by the courts.

Pike stated that she had been prepared to die on the night of the scheduled execution. Expressing gratitude to medical staff, attorneys, and global supporters for their kindness, she noted she is attempting to heal one day at a time while searching for meaning in her survival.
