Marty Erlichman, the entertainment manager who discovered Barbra Streisand and shaped her historic career for over sixty years, has passed away at the age of 97. 

## Discovery and Partnership

Erlichman first encountered Streisand while attending a performance by comedian Phil Leeds, where the eighteen-year-old future superstar appeared as an opening act. Recalling the evening years later, the manager noted that her performance left him with continuous chills. Shortly thereafter, Streisand contacted him for representation while playing a club engagement in Detroit. Erlichman traveled to meet her, securing a deal that marked the beginning of a lifelong professional association. Notably, the pair maintained their decades-long partnership without ever signing a formal contract.

Throughout their collaboration, Erlichman managed recording, film, television, theater, and concert agreements. He executive produced ten of her television specials—beginning with *My Name Is Barbra* in 1963 through *Barbra: The Music… The Mem’ries… The Magic!* in 2017—and oversaw numerous concert tours, including her 2000 Millennium Concert and 2006–2007 world tour. Streisand paid tribute to her late manager, describing him as a constant, irreplaceable presence by her side through every endeavor.

## Producing and Other Ventures

Beyond his work with Streisand, Erlichman accumulated several film producing credits, starting with the 1974 feature *For Pete’s Sake*. His subsequent film projects included the Michael Crichton thriller *Coma*, an American adaptation of *Breathless*, and multiple films in the *Ernest* comedy series.

Erlichman also managed a diverse roster of clients throughout his career, including heavyweight boxing champion George Foreman, comedian Chevy Chase, record producer Bob Crewe, pop group Gary Puckett & The Union Gap, and the Irish folk ensemble The Clancy Brothers and Tommy Makem.

He is survived by his wife of 52 years, Miko Mayama Erlichman, his son J.J., daughter-in-law Talia, and four grandchildren. A private service is planned, and the family has requested that donations be directed to St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital or the National Kidney Foundation in lieu of flowers.
