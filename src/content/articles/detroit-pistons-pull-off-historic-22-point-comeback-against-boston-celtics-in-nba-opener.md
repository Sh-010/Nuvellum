## Pistons overcome 22-point deficit

The Detroit Pistons opened the new NBA season in dramatic fashion on Tuesday, erasing a 22-point deficit to defeat the Boston Celtics 121-113. The victory marked the largest come-from-behind win against Boston in the last thirty seasons, fueled by a remarkable 23-0 scoring run during the fourth quarter.

Boston had previously built their largest lead early in the third quarter at 72-50, driven by rapid scoring from Paul George. However, Detroit narrowed the gap to six points by the end of the third period before completely overtaking their opponents in the final frame.

## Standout individual performances

Javonte Green energized the Detroit comeback by scoring 19 points after remaining benched during the entire first half. Green hit a crucial go-ahead three-pointer in the fourth quarter, followed by key plays from teammates Jalen Duren and Duncan Robinson that stretched the lead to 107-99 with just over five minutes remaining.

Cade Cunningham and Jalen Duren each contributed 22 points for the Pistons, with Duren adding nine rebounds. Duncan Robinson finished with 21 points, sinking five of nine attempts from beyond the arc, while Ausar Thompson recorded a double-double with 11 points and 11 rebounds.

For Boston, debutant Paul George put up 38 points, setting a record for the most points scored by a Celtic in a debut appearance. Meanwhile, teammate Jayson Tatum struggled from the field, missing 17 of his 23 shots to finish with 13 points and 11 assists.
