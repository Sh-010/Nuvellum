Actor Barry Keoghan appeared in a social media video shared by United Kingdom celebrity facial expert Nilam Holmes to document his second Xerf skin-tightening procedure. 

## Skin Treatment and Holmes Partnership

The non-invasive procedure utilizes radio-frequency technology rather than injections to renew collagen and tighten skin, with face and neck sessions priced around $3,500. Holmes, the founder of Dermaspa, stated in the accompanying caption that she has managed the Oscar nominee's skin across various projects and campaigns. She noted that Keoghan volunteered to be filmed after she fell behind schedule on content creation.

In the video, Keoghan described the session as relaxing and therapeutic, stating that the upkeep improves his appearance and self-perception while emphasizing the importance of self-care. Holmes previously detailed that the actor's demanding schedule involving film productions, interviews, and press obligations often leads to irregular routines and disrupted sleep that impact his skin.

## Addressing Online Abuse

The procedure comes months after Keoghan opened up about facing severe online harassment regarding his appearance. In an interview earlier in the year, he revealed that the digital hostility became so intense that it caused him to shy away from public outings and retreat inward. Although he stepped away from social platforms to avoid negative remarks, he admitted that checking reactions after public events frequently yielded unpleasant responses.
