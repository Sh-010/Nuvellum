## Early dominance establishes control

Middlesbrough claimed the top position in the Sky Bet Championship standings following a comprehensive victory against Bolton Wanderers at the Toughsheet Community Stadium. The visitors initiated the attack immediately, earning an early corner kick before taking the lead after 13 minutes. Amario Cozier-Duberry delivered a pass to Aidan Morris, who converted the chance.

Bolton goalkeeper Gavin Bazunu managed to block several attempts, but Middlesbrough doubled their advantage shortly after the half-hour mark through Will Lankshear. A third goal followed before halftime when Adilson Malanda headed a corner toward the goal, resulting in an unfortunate deflection off Lewis Brunt.

## Bolton response and late goals

Despite trailing by three, the hosts responded before the interval when Theo Bair scored through a crowded penalty area. Bolton maintained defensive pressure following the restart, preventing their opponents from recording a shot for nearly twenty minutes.

However, Middlesbrough reasserted control as the match progressed. Bazunu stopped additional efforts before Cozier-Duberry extended the lead with a high shot following a pass from Riley McGree. Lankshear added his second and the team's fifth goal late in the contest, capitalizing on another unselfish pass from McGree during a counterattack.

## Standings impact

The win lifts Kim Hellberg's squad two points clear at the top of the league table ahead of upcoming fixtures for rival clubs.
