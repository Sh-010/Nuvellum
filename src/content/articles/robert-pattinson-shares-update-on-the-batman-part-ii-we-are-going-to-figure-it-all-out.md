## Production pause and status update

Actor Robert Pattinson has spoken out regarding the temporary production pause on *The Batman Part II*. Speaking to Official Pop Drop while promoting *Primetime*, Pattinson addressed the future of the Matt Reeves-directed superhero film.

"Yeah, we're pausing for a little bit, and then we're going to figure it all out," Pattinson told the outlet.

The studio previously announced that production on the DC Studios project was temporarily halted while director Matt Reeves focuses on a family matter. DC Studios stated that Reeves has their full support as they look toward continuing the production.

## Cast and sequel expectations

The upcoming sequel is scheduled to feature returning cast members Robert Pattinson as Batman, Andy Serkis as Alfred, and Colin Farrell as Penguin. They are joined by new additions to the franchise including Scarlett Johansson, Sebastian Stan, Brian Tyree Henry, Jayme Lawson, Charles Dance, Gil Perez-Abraham, and Sebastian Koch.

Earlier this year during the premiere of *The Drama*, Pattinson described the sequel's script as extraordinary and indicated that the movie would differ significantly from the 2022 original film. He noted that the production was taking "big swings."

As of now, *The Batman Part II* remains on the release calendar for February 18, 2028.
