## A distinctive feature on the pitch

With hair reaching his ankles, 36-year-old Anguilla forward Aedan Scipio is notable for his lengthy dreadlocks, which he keeps tied to his body using a band around his waist. However, his standout feature proved to be an operational hazard during a Concacaf Nations League match against Antigua and Barbuda at the Sir Vivian Richards Stadium.

Scipio entered the game as a substitute in the 69th minute, with Anguilla trailing 5-0. 

## Red cards for hair-pulling

It took only three minutes after Scipio's arrival for opponent Keon Greene to receive a straight red card for tugging the forward's dreadlocks.

Later in the match, during added time, Antigua and Barbuda were reduced to nine players when Shalon Knight was also sent off after pulling Scipio's dreadlocks from behind to take him off his feet.

Anguilla currently sits 210th in Fifa's international rankings, with only San Marino placed below them.
