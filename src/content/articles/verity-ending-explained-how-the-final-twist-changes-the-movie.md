Directed by Michael Showalter and based on a novel by Colleen Hoover, the psychological thriller *Verity* features a cast that includes Anne Hathaway, Dakota Johnson, and Josh Hartnett. The film follows struggling writer Lowen Ashleigh (Johnson), who is hired by Jeremy Crawford (Hartnett) to complete the book series written by his famous wife, Verity Crawford (Hathaway). The job comes with a substantial sum of money and a place to live in the Crawfords' remote Vermont mansion, where a paralyzed Verity also resides following a car crash.

## The hidden manuscript and the murder

While working in the home, Lowen discovers a hidden manuscript written by Verity. The text outlines dark secrets, including resentment toward her twin children and an admission that she orchestrated the boating accident that caused her daughter's death. 

In the third act, Lowen uses a baby monitor in Verity's bedroom to discover that Verity has been faking the severity of her injuries. After reading the manuscript, Jeremy and Lowen believe Verity is dangerous. Jeremy kills her in a rage, and Lowen advises him on how to prevent police suspicion before they dump the body in a lake.

## The final twist and discovery

A year later, Jeremy and Lowen are expecting a child, their joint book release is successful, and they prepare to move out of the Vermont house. While packing, Lowen finds a letter hidden under a floorboard in her son Crew's bedroom. 

The letter, written by Verity and addressed to her son, reveals that the shocking manuscript was actually a writing exercise in antagonistic journaling, written from a villain's perspective to help develop characters for her novels. Furthermore, the letter claims that Jeremy had been drugging Verity, causing the car crash, and that Verity faked her paralysis for her own safety while waiting for an opportunity to leave with their son.

The film concludes as Lowen hides the letter, wipes away her tears, and heads downstairs to leave the house with Jeremy, leaving the true nature of the characters reframed by the final revelation.
