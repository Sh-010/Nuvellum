## Speed limits and Starlink connectivity

Elon Musk has suggested that the United States government may be behind restrictions on the speed at which certain Ukrainian weapons can travel while maintaining a connection to SpaceX's Starlink internet network.

Starlink serves as a tool enabling Kyiv to guide drones to targets and protect its airspace. However, unexpected speed limits were introduced, disrupting battlefield operations. Musk wrote on social media that Starshield, a military-grade sister service to Starlink, is controlled by the US government, stating that any limits on speed or usage are decided by the US military and ultimately the US president.

Ukrainian officials, including President Volodymyr Zelensky, had appealed for connectivity to be restored. Starlink restricted speed limits to 100mph (160km/h), causing weapons travelling faster than that threshold to lose network connectivity. The restrictions were partially restored by Wednesday evening, according to former defence minister Mykhailo Fedorov.

Musk defended his company's involvement by writing that Starshield systems are owned and controlled by the US government. He added that Starlink is intended for civilian or defensive activities and cannot be used for offensive military action, noting that doing so would be inappropriate for a commercial enterprise. The US government has not commented on the matter.

## Context and political strains

The implementation of the speed limits coincided with Russian military strikes against Ukraine that killed several people in Kyiv and affected communications and energy facilities used by the military. Zelensky described the action as one of the largest attacks from Russia, which left at least 600,000 people across Ukraine without electricity.

An adviser to Zelensky stated that the restrictions could not be considered a coincidence and were linked to ongoing strains between Kyiv and Washington. US President Donald Trump has expressed frustration regarding Ukrainian strikes on Russian energy facilities, though Trump denied threatening to restrict intelligence sharing if Ukraine did not agree to an energy ceasefire.

Musk also commented on leadership in Ukraine on social media, writing regarding the conflict and suggesting a change in leadership after similar remarks by Trump. Under martial law following Russia's 2022 invasion, elections in Ukraine have been suspended.

During a visit to London, Zelensky met with UK Prime Andy Burnham and King Charles. Downing Street announced expedited delivery of lightweight multirole missiles to Ukraine for use against incoming drones.
