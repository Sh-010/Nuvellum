Actor Rohan Campbell is preparing to transition behind the camera, revealing plans to direct his own feature film next summer.

## Directorial ambitions

Speaking to Film Updates, Campbell shared that he is currently working toward a summer 2026 timeframe to helm the project, which he described as a cute idea and a significant current focus. "I'm trying to direct my own movie in the summer of next year, which I'm really excited about," Campbell said. "It's a cute idea, so we're sort of just trying to get all systems to go on that one... It's a big part of my focus, and it's a very personal story." The project is currently awaiting an official greenlight.

## Acting background

Campbell began his onscreen acting career at the age of 10. He gained wider recognition as Frank Hardy in Hulu's series *The Hardy Boys*, which broadcast across three seasons from 2020 to 2023. 

He has subsequently appeared in several genre films, including *Halloween Ends* in 2022, alongside roles in *The Monkey* and *Silent Night, Deadly Night* in 2025. His recent credits include the Netflix series *Below*, where he portrays Jay Penney, the troubled Newfoundland expat and son of fisherman Calvin Penney, played by Josh Hartnett.
