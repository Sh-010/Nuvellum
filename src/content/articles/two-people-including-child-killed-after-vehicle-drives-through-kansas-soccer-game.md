Two people, including a child, were killed when a person drove a vehicle through a soccer game at the Stryker soccer complex in Wichita, Kansas, according to police confirmations. Multiple other individuals sustained injuries during the incident.

## Driver in custody

Aaron Moses of the Wichita police department stated during a Saturday news conference that the driver of the vehicle is in custody. Authorities reported that two juveniles were inside the vehicle at the time.

Police confirmed that one child died at the scene alongside one adult. Names and ages have been withheld pending family notification.

## Investigation underway

Describing the event as a tragedy, Moses noted that officials are continuing their investigation. In response to the incident, the city announced that all events at the Stryker soccer complex are canceled until further notice.
