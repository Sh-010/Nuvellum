Prime Video's *Carrie* series concludes its first season with an ambiguous sequence during its prom scene, prompting showrunner Mike Flanagan to clarify the events in an interview.

## Unraveling the prom massacre sequence

In the series, character Emaline (Thalia Dudek) befriends Carrie (Summer Howell) and helps her use psychic abilities. It is later revealed that Emaline is astral projecting from afar, making her invisible to everyone except Carrie. 

During the prom, after Carrie is crowned queen and drenched in pig's blood, she unleashes her powers, destroying smartphones and accidentally killing her prom date, Tommy Ross (Joel Oulette), while targeting people fleeing from a catwalk. Emaline then appears onstage behind Carrie, seemingly taking control of her body to unleash widespread mayhem across the school.

Later in the episode, Carrie tracks down and murders her tormentor Chris Hargensen (Alison Thornton) and Chris' boyfriend, Billy Nolan (Arthur Conti), while Emaline is absent.

## Defining character culpability

Flanagan explained that certain actions were meant to be solely Carrie White's responsibility. According to the showrunner, Carrie is entirely to blame for killing Tommy Ross, Billy Nolan, and Chris Hargensen, noting that Emaline was positioned away from Carrie during the confrontation with Chris.

Conversely, Flanagan indicated that other deaths during the sequence belong to Emaline. He stated that the creative team did not believe the character of Carrie would consciously choose to kill Principal Grayle (Matthew Lillard), Ms. D (Amber Midthunder), or ignite innocent bystanders.

## Exploring themes of tragedy

Flanagan emphasized that the series aimed to explore the unintended consequences of cruelty rather than serve as a metaphor for school shootings. He described the narrative approach as treating the event as a tragedy with multiple authors, reflecting a collection of small decisions by various people that led to the final outcome.
