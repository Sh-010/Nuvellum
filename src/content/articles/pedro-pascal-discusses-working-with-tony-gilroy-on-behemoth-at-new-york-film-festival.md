Following the world premiere of the drama *Behemoth!* at the New York Film Festival, actor Pedro Pascal shared his initial impressions of working with writer-director Tony Gilroy.

## World premiere at the New York Film Festival

Speaking at a post-screening Q&A, Pascal expressed surprise at the intimate and healing nature of the project. The Golden Globe-nominated actor admitted that he initially expected Gilroy to be intimidating given his history with complex storytelling.

"You have created this complex Jason Bourne-like puzzle over something that is so intimate and so healing this mysterious way," Pascal told Gilroy during the event. Pascal added that he anticipated Gilroy would be "kinda mean," while noting that Gilroy had expected him to be a dork.

## Plot and cast details

In *Behemoth!*, Pascal stars as Alex Serian, a former child prodigy and cellist who returns home to Los Angeles to compose film scores and reconnect with his family.

Written and directed by Gilroy, the Searchlight Pictures film is scheduled to premiere in theaters on December 4. The ensemble cast also features Olivia Wilde, Eva Victor, Will Arnett, Hank Azaria, Margarita Levieva, and Matthew Lillard.
