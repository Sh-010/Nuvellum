International sales firm EST N8 has acquired Marco Balsamo's soccer drama "Forza" and will introduce the feature to buyers at the Tokyo International Film Festival's TIFFCOM market, running from October 28 to 30. The film's first public screening is scheduled for October 25 at Jakarta Film Week.

## Plot and Cast

The feature centers on Bima, an 11-year-old from Bali who aspires to a professional football career to support his family. His life shifts after forming a bond with a former AC Milan player nearing the end of his career. Newcomer Ghazi Alhabsyi leads the cast alongside director Balsamo, Tara Sibel Demren, and AC Milan icon Alessandro Costacurta. Indonesian Film Festival winners Atiqah Hasiholan, Oka Antara, and Tio Pakusadewo also appear.

## Production and Release

Balsamo wrote the script and produced the film alongside Demren for Riva Studios, with "The Raid" franchise producer Ario Sagantoro also on board. Adhya Pictures has scheduled the film's theatrical release in Indonesia for 2027. Ahead of its festival screening, online tickets sold out within five minutes.

Sophie Shi, head of studios at EST N8, described the project as a family film bridging Indonesia and Italy. Balsamo noted that the production represents a long-standing collaboration for independent filmmaking based in Indonesia.
