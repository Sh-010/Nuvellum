Square Enix has released three role-playing games on the Nintendo Switch 2 over a two-week period, including *Dragon Quest 11S*, *Octopath Traveler*, and *Octopath Traveler 2*. 

## Release details and missing upgrade options

The publisher launched the titles without providing an upgrade path or a way to carry save files over from previous versions. Because of this, reviewers note that the ports are difficult to recommend for players who already own the titles on older hardware. 

## Dragon Quest 11S improvements

Among the releases, *Dragon Quest 11S* shows the most noticeable visual and performance differences compared to its original Switch counterpart. The older version featured low-resolution textures and a blurry appearance, whereas the Switch 2 edition renders the world of Erdrea with brighter, clearer graphics. 

Players can also select a 60fps performance mode, though shorter load times and resolution shifts remain visible between handheld and docked modes. Analysts suggest the new version is primarily suited for first-time players or those willing to purchase the game a second time.

## Octopath Traveler ports and performance

The original *Octopath Traveler* and its sequel, *Octopath Traveler 2*, received more modest updates on the new hardware. While the high-definition 2D pixel art style remains similar to earlier releases, the Switch 2 editions benefit from reduced load times. For *Octopath Traveler 2*, loading from the home screen to the title page is cut nearly in half to under 20 seconds, compared to nearly 40 seconds on the original Switch.
