Disney has revealed the official release schedule and episode titles for the third season of *Percy Jackson and the Olympians* during an appearance at New York Comic Con. The upcoming season introduces a significant change to the show's rollout by moving away from traditional weekly releases.

## Release schedule and format

Instead of the one-episode-per-week model used in previous seasons, season 3 will adopt a two-episode-per-week format. Because the third season consists of eight episodes, the entire run will be broadcast over the course of a single month, starting on November 20.

The full schedule and episode titles announced by Disney include:

* **Nov. 20:** Episode 1 — "My Rescue Operation Goes Very Wrong" and Episode 2 — "An Old Dead Friend Comes to Visit"
* **Nov. 27:** Episode 3 — "I Learn How to Grow Zombies" and Episode 4 — "We Visit the Junkyard of the Gods"
* **Dec. 4:** Episode 5 — "I Have a Dam Problem" and Episode 6 — "I Wrestle Santa’s Evil Twin"
* **Dec. 11:** Episode 7 — "I Put on A Few Million Extra Pounds" and Episode 8 — "The Gods Vote How to Kill Us"

## Story and casting

Season 3 adapts *The Titan's Curse*, serving as a turning point in the Rick Riordan book series. The plot centers on Percy, played by Walker Scobell, as he searches for the missing goddess Athena and his friend Annabeth, played by Leah Sava Jeffries, while navigating a conflict between the gods and the titans.

The new season will also introduce characters such as Nico di Angelo, played by Levi Chrisopulos, and Rachel Elizabeth Dare.

## Netflix availability and fan response

Ahead of the season 3 premiere, Disney placed the first two seasons of the series on Netflix under a temporary three-month licensing deal. Following its arrival on the platform, the show reached the fifth spot among the most popular TV shows on Netflix, according to FlixPatrol data.

While Disney did not announce a further renewal during New York Comic Con, some fans have expressed concerns regarding the future adaptation of the remaining books. Disney previously renewed the series for its third season in March 2025, ahead of the season 2 release in December.
