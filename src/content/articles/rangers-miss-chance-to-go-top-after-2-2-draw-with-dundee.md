## Missed opportunity at Dens Park

Rangers missed the chance to move to the summit of the Scottish Premiership following a gripping 2-2 draw against Dundee at Dens Park.

The Ibrox side travelled to Dundee on a run of seven consecutive victories, but manager Derek McInnes lamented a costly second-half mistake that halted their momentum. After Bradley Fink put the hosts ahead in a disjointed first half for Rangers, Badredine Bouanani equalised just before the interval.

Rangers raised their intensity after the break, taking the lead through a long-range strike from Nicolas Raskin. However, a mishit backpass by Dan Neil to goalkeeper Ivor Pandur allowed Callum Jones to pounce and secure a draw for Dundee.

"We've made a mistake for Dundee's equaliser and it was like a balloon popping," McInnes told BBC Scotland. "We then had to dig deep and go again, and we never quite managed to get to the same levels."

## Performance levels and missed chances

McInnes expressed frustration with his side's performance over the full 90 minutes, noting that a strong 25-minute spell after half-time was insufficient.

"When we play in that manner, we're a very good team," McInnes said. Former Rangers midfielder Derek Ferguson echoed those concerns on BBC Sportsound, stating that brief periods of dominance are not enough to sustain a title challenge.

## Profligacy concerns

Beyond the defensive error, McInnes highlighted a broader issue with wastefulness in front of goal. Rangers' expected goals (xG) rating at Dens Park approached two, reflecting the goals they scored, but the team has underperformed significantly over the course of the season.

Statistically, the Ibrox side have scored 13 league goals from a total accumulated xG of just under 20, marking an underperformance of nearly seven goals. With a difficult fixture list approaching, McInnes stressed that his team must become more clinical.

"We had so many better chances than [Raskin's goal] to take the game away from Dundee," McInnes told Sky Sports. "It's the same kind of theme for me a lot of the time. We've had dominant performances but not really had the goals to show for it."
