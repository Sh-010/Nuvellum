The BBC has provided a first look at its upcoming crime drama series *The Last Detectives*, created and written by Matthew Barry. Originally titled *Old Town Murders*, the series is produced by Nicola Shindler’s Quay Street Productions and centers on a team of investigators navigating murder cases against the backdrop of Tenby in Wales.

## Cast and characters

The newly released images introduce the central investigative partnership of DI Glyn Walsh, played by Mark Lewis-Jones, and DS Sion Dearden, played by Steffan Rhodri. The detective team also includes DC Remy Jones (Bethan-Mary James), DC Neil Miller (Jordan Davies), and forensic biologist Pamela Thomas (Julie Graham), alongside Catherine Ayers.

Barry described the project as a story exploring friendship and meaning in later life through a police procedural framework, expressing enthusiasm for bringing the characters and the Tenby setting to audiences.

## Production and creative team

*The Last Detectives* marks Shindler’s first production since *Tip Toe* and reunites several team members who previously collaborated on the BBC single drama *Men Up*. Executive producers include Davina Earl and Nicola Shindler for Quay Street Productions, alongside Rebecca Ferguson for the BBC. Nick Andrews serves as co-commissioner for BBC Wales.

Alongside Barry, who writes and executive produces, Hannah Daniel wrote episode four. Andy Newbery directs the first four episodes and serves as executive producer, while Rhys Carter directs episodes five and six. Guy Hescott is the series producer. ITV Studios handles international sales, and the production received support from the Welsh Government via Creative Wales, with filming taking place in Tenby, Cardiff, and along the South Wales Coastline.
