## Emotional tribute at the Hollywood Bowl

Miley Cyrus paid a tearful tribute to her godmother, the late country music icon Dolly Parton, during a concert at the Hollywood Bowl. Sitting on the edge of the stage, Cyrus shared memories with the crowd, noting she had only managed to see Parton perform live in concert once in her entire life, and that single performance took place at the exact same venue.

Cyrus recounted that during the night she watched Parton perform at the Hollywood Bowl, her idol had opened the show by singing the theme song from *Hannah Montana* after realizing Cyrus was in the audience. "I feel her now, more than ever," Cyrus told the crowd, adding that she misses her.

## Performance of "Jolene" and extensive setlist

Following her remarks, Cyrus performed Parton’s classic song "Jolene." According to *Rolling Stone*, the concert setlist also featured a broad selection of Cyrus' own material, including "Orbit," "Different Religion," "Bass Persuades," "Handstand," "Something Beautiful," "Walk of Fame," "Redlights," "Do You Realize??," "Karen Don’t Be Sad," "We Can’t Stop," "Wrecking Ball," "Snow," "Used to Be Young," "The Climb," "Party in the U.S.A.," "Let’s Get Married," and "Flowers."

## Previous tributes

Cyrus previously honored Parton following the icon's death in August. At that time, she wrote that she would always love Parton and strive to make her proud, expressing heartbreak for Parton's family, friends, and the rest of the world.
