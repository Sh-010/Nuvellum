Following fan concern over footage from the Beach Boys' concert in Cincinnati, actor and musician John Stamos has issued a statement regarding frontman Mike Love's health. Stamos acknowledged that Love is facing serious health challenges while continuing to perform.

## Fan concern and recent performances

Fans expressed alarm after viewing social media footage from the group's Saturday night show in Ohio, where Love remained seated and motionless throughout the performance without taking his usual lead vocals. Stamos, who has played with Love for 41 years, posted a statement on Instagram ahead of a scheduled Sunday performance at Nashville's Ryman Auditorium.

"I’ve played with Mike for 41 years. I never imagined how deeply it would affect me to see someone larger than life face something so human and fragile," Stamos wrote. "We hope he’ll be doing what he loves for years to come, but we don’t know what the future holds. We have tonight."

## Tour schedule and future outlook

The Beach Boys had previously canceled several months of tour dates starting in July while Love addressed health concerns. Following a private performance, the group returned to public touring in Cincinnati. 

The band's upcoming itinerary includes shows in Orlando on November 11 and Bristol on November 13, followed by a single publicly announced 2027 gig. The reduced touring schedule has led followers to question whether the group is preparing to wind down Love's time fronting the band.

In his statement, Stamos praised Love's dedication and resilience, noting that despite urging from doctors, family, and friends to focus on his health, Love insists on performing because he draws energy from his band, audience, and fellow musicians.
