An Israeli air strike on a vehicle in Gaza City's Tuffah neighbourhood killed one person and wounded several others, according to emergency services. In central Gaza's az-Zawayda, a four-year-old boy, Osama Ahmed Abed, died from wounds sustained in an Israeli attack three weeks prior, reported the Palestinian news agency Wafa. His death raised the recorded Palestinian death toll in Gaza since October 7, 2023, to over 74,382.

Gaza's Civil Defence agency expressed deep concern regarding forced displacement near the "Yellow Line," the boundary established under the October 2025 ceasefire. The agency reported that heavy explosions and indiscriminate gunfire damaged displaced persons' tents and previously weakened structures, prompting an urgent appeal to halt operations against the civilian population.

## Territorial control and ceasefire status

Nearly two-thirds of Gaza remains under Israeli military control or access restrictions, according to Al Jazeera's mapping. The "Yellow Line" was designated under a 20-point plan to end hostilities, though further planned withdrawals did not occur. The US-backed Board of Peace commemorated the ceasefire's anniversary by stating that former US President Donald Trump's leadership ended an open-ended conflict, while reports noted that at least 1,400 Palestinians have been killed since the truce began.

## Raids in the occupied West Bank

In the occupied West Bank cities of Ramallah and el-Bireh, Israeli operations wounded a child with live ammunition and caused tear gas suffocation among several residents, the Palestinian Ministry of Health reported. Wafa reported that Palestine TV correspondent Khaled Mattawa sustained a hand injury from a sound bomb, and Sky News Arabia correspondent Firas Lutfi was wounded in the leg by shrapnel.

Israeli forces also conducted raids on the offices of multiple Palestinian non-governmental organisations and the Palestine Medical Complex courtyard. Human rights lawyer Muhammad Dahleh stated that authorities utilize military orders and classified evidence to shut down Palestinian rights groups. 

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu stated in a video message that Israel was preparing for a scenario where Palestinian Authority forces might turn against Israel, asserting that no military force other than the Israeli military would operate west of the Jordan River and that Israel would extend its control across the territory.
