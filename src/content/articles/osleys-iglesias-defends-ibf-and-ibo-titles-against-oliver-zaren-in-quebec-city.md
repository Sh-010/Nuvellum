## Championship bout details

Elite southpaw boxers Osleys Iglesias and Oliver Zaren meet in a 12-round clash for the IBF and IBO super middleweight title belts. Promoted by Eye of the Tiger Management, the event takes place on Thursday, October 8, at Capitole de Québec in Quebec City, Canada.

Cuban boxer Osleys Iglesias enters the matchup defending his championship status with a 15-0-0 record, while Danish challenger Oliver Zaren brings a 19-0-1 record to the ring. The event begins at 7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT, with ringwalks for the main event anticipated around 11 p.m. ET / 8 p.m. PT.
