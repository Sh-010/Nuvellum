Square Enix is continuing its trend of revisiting 1990s titles with the upcoming release of *Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United*. Following previous projects such as the HD-2D remake of *Live A Live* and the 3D remake *Dragon Quest 7 Reimagined*, the publisher's latest offering is a revamp of a Super Nintendo Entertainment System game recognized for its non-linear narrative, multiple protagonists, and unconventional minigames.

## Combat mechanics and progression

The title features a turn-based combat system that bypasses traditional experience points in favor of stat and health increases at the conclusion of battles. Character builds are shaped by actions repeated during combat, while new abilities are unlocked through the Glimmer system, indicated by light-bulb icons in the combat menu. Players must manage limited skill slots, replacing older abilities with more powerful options.

Health regenerates automatically after battles, but Battle Points (BP) used for skills and magic do not. If a party member's HP drops to zero, they can be revived with standard healing items at the cost of a Life Point (LP). Characters who lose all of their LP before reaching an inn or recovery point are permanently removed from the roster.

## Party composition and release details

Players can assemble a party of up to five members, consisting of one of eight main characters alongside four optional recruits. The preview build showcased party members including Khalid, a swordsman; Robin; and Flurry, a snowman character. According to the preview, the game features various other unusual optional recruits, such as a lobster-person and a character named Fat Robin.

Co-developed by Square Enix and Xeen Inc., *Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies United* is scheduled for release on Feb. 16, 2027, for Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X.
