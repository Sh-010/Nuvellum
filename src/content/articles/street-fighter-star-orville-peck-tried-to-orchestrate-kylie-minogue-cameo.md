## Attempting a cameo

Musician and actor Orville Peck attempted to arrange for his friend and collaborator Kylie Minogue to make a cameo appearance in the latest *Street Fighter* feature adaptation, in which Peck stars as Vega. 

Speaking to Queerty, Peck explained that production members were initially having difficulty reaching the pop icon. Recognizing an opportunity, Peck told the crew he could contact her directly. According to Peck, Minogue was interested in participating, but scheduling conflicts ultimately prevented the cameo from happening.

## A connection to the 1994 film

Minogue previously portrayed Cammy in the original 1994 live-action adaptation of the video game. Peck shared that he informed Minogue immediately after securing his role in the new Paramount film. 

Minogue told Peck that filming the 1994 adaptation was one of the most fun experiences of her life and expressed enthusiasm for his involvement in the new project directed by Kitao Sakurai. 

Peck noted that he is still attempting to arrange a meeting between Minogue and Mel Jarnson, the actress who plays Cammy in the current adaptation. He also hinted that a cameo could potentially materialize in a future sequel.
