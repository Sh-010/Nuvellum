## Expanding the franchise into scripted horror

The unscripted television format *Fear Factor* is transitioning into a scripted narrative for the first time. Fox Entertainment Studios and Endemol Shine North America, a Banijay America company, are partnering with Holywater Tech to launch an untitled vertical spinoff on the My Drama platform.

Created specifically for mobile devices, the upcoming project will comprise 30 three-minute episodes. Rather than following the reality format, the production shifts the intellectual property into a fictional horror narrative.

## Plot and creative team

The narrative centers on preparation week for a remote horror television production. During prep, a practical-effects crew accidentally activates a cursed fortune-telling device. The machine subjects the crew to individualized fatal trials, compelling survivors to investigate its history before facing further losses.

Thom Woodley and Andrew Beck wrote the series, while Gabriel Caste serves as director. Ian Nsenga and Yuliia Vyshnevska are attached as producers. Fox Entertainment Global will handle worldwide distribution.

## Strategic mobile expansion

Hannah Pillemer, Head of Scripted at Fox Entertainment Studios, noted that the network's recent unscripted revival created an opportunity to push the brand into uncharted territory. Michael Heyerman, Executive Vice President of Unscripted Series at Endemol Shine North America, added that the vertical format matches the immediacy and serialized nature of the brand.

The announcement follows a second-season renewal for the reality competition series *Fear Factor: House of Fear*, hosted by Johnny Knoxville and produced by Fox. No release date has been finalized for the upcoming mobile adaptation.
