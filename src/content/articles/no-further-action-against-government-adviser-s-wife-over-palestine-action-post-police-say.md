## Police assessment concluded

Police have stated that no further action will be taken against Sarah Helm, the wife of the government's national security adviser, following a social media post referencing Palestine Action.

Helm, who is married to Jonathan Powell, posted on X on Thursday, writing: "God help us authors who support Palestine and Palestine Action." Her post was a response to a discussion concerning author Sally Rooney, whose books will not be supplied to booksellers in the UK after she announced plans to direct her royalties toward the proscribed organization.

## Investigation details

The Metropolitan Police reported on Saturday that Helm's post was evaluated by specialist officers who concluded it "did not meet the criminal threshold." The force confirmed that no additional police action would follow.

On Friday, the Metropolitan Police had stated that the post was undergoing assessment to decide if further investigation was necessary after officers became aware of the message published on Thursday, 15 October.

Palestine Action was proscribed as a terrorist organization in July 2025. Providing support to the group constitutes a criminal offense that carries a maximum penalty of 14 years in imprisonment. Author Sally Rooney stated she would contribute earnings from her books to the group, which describes its goal as ending global participation in Israel's regime. Consequently, publisher Faber announced it could not sell her novels, such as *Normal People* and *Conversations with Friends*, within the United Kingdom.
