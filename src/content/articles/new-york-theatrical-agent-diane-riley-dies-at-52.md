## Career and industry leadership

Diane Riley, a prominent New York theatrical agent and partner at Harden Curtis Kirsten Riley, died unexpectedly on September 16 in Asbury Park, New Jersey, at the age of 52. Her family and agency announced that her passing followed complications from surgery, concluding a period of declining health over the preceding year and a half.

Riley started her professional journey in the performing arts as an assistant company manager at the Goodspeed Opera House before transitioning to casting roles. In 1997, she joined Harden Curtis Associates. She advanced through the organization to become an agent and eventually achieved partner status in 2017 alongside Michael Kirsten, leading to the firm's rebranding.

## Client roster and community impact

Throughout her career, Riley maintained a notable client roster that included prominent stage and screen performers such as Lisa Emery, Curtiss Cook, Andy Mientus, Christiane Noll, and Thom Sesma. Beyond her agency work, she contributed to the broader industry as Vice President of the Board of Directors for the National Association of Talent Representatives.

Her firm remembered her for bringing an infectious energy and dry wit to everything she did. A celebration of life is scheduled for Friday afternoon, October 30, at New World Stages in Manhattan. She is survived by her husband, father, and brother.
