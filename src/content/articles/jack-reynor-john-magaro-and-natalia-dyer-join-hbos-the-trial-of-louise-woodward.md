## Cast additions for HBO true-crime series

Jack Reynor, John Magaro, and Natalia Dyer have been added to the cast of HBO's upcoming true-crime limited series *The Trial of Louise Woodward*. Created by Matthew Barry, the series focuses on the 1997 case of an 18-year-old British au pair, played by Catriona Chandler, who was accused of shaking to death a baby left in her care by her American host family. The trial sparked an international media firestorm as it unfolded in public view in the United Kingdom and the United States.

Reynor has been cast as Gerry Leone, the lead prosecutor in the trial. Magaro will play lead defense counsel Barry Scheck, while Dyer joins as Ada Lynn Rooney, a Middlesex County assistant district attorney.

## Extended cast and production details

The trio joins an ensemble cast that includes Joanne Froggatt as Susan Woodward, the mother of Louise, and Meghann Fahy and Sagar Radia as Deborah and Sunil Eappen, the parents of the victim, Matthew Eappen. Chloë Sevigny portrays defense attorney Elaine Whitfield Sharp, and Hugh Laurie stars as Judge Hiller B. Zobel, who presided over the murder trial.

Matthew Barry serves as writer and showrunner, and is executive producing alongside Susanne Bier, who is directing all episodes. Additional executive producers include Frank Spotnitz, Emily Feller, Tobi de Graaff, and Antoine Douaihy, with Big Light Productions and Anton TV producing the project.
