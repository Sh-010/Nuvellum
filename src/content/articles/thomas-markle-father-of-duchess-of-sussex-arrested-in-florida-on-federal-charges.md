## Arrest and federal charges

Thomas Markle, the 82-year-old father of Meghan, the Duchess of Sussex, was taken into custody by federal agents in Florida. According to the Federal Bureau of Investigation, the arrest occurred at a nursing home near Tampa. United States prosecutors stated he is charged with one count of possessing child sexual abuse material and faces up to 20 years in federal prison if convicted.

A federal criminal complaint alleges that law enforcement seized roughly 78 items from two Los Angeles-area storage units rented by Markle for nearly twenty years. Court documents indicate the recovered items included photographs, slides, film negatives, and books containing illegal material. Prosecutors stated the investigation involves allegations that Markle kept explicit photographs of himself with prepubescent girls.

## Prior investigations and family response

Authorities in the Philippines previously initiated an investigation into Markle concerning similar allegations. British police also stated they would refer previous claims to overseas law enforcement after discovering they had not been forwarded when initially received.

In response to earlier reports regarding material found at her father's former residence, the Duchess of Sussex released a statement indicating she was deeply distressed by the allegations and had previously distanced herself from him. A spokesperson for the duchess did not immediately provide comment following the recent arrest. Markle has previously denied the accusations, calling them untrue and sick lies, and answered negatively when asked by reporters if he was a pedophile.
