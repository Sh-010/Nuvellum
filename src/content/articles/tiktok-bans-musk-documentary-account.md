## Account closure ahead of release

TikTok has removed the social media account promoting Alex Gibney's documentary about Elon Musk from its platform, just ahead of the film's wider release. According to The Hollywood Reporter, the film's distributor, Bleecker Street, was informed on a Friday afternoon that a permanent suspension of the account was imminent.

TikTok did not immediately respond to requests for comment regarding the ban, and Bleecker Street has not yet responded to inquiries from outside sources.

## Wider advertising rejections

The ban follows previous friction between major technology platforms and the documentary's promotional campaign. Last month, YouTube, Meta, X, and TikTok all faced backlash after rejecting advertisements for the film. While YouTube and Google quickly reversed their decisions and characterized the rejection as an error, TikTok and X maintained their positions and have continued to refuse paid advertisements for the documentary.

Larry Ellison, part owner of TikTok, appears in the film via a text message offering to help Musk fund his acquisition of Twitter. TikTok has no official affiliation with Elon Musk.

The removal of the promotional profile marks an escalation in the platform's restrictions against the film. Shortly before the account was taken down, it published a post advising users to see the movie before it was shut down.
