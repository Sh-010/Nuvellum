Filmmaker Rupert Russell is developing a new feature documentary titled "Mad Dad," offering an intimate look at his father, the celebrated British director Ken Russell. The announcement coincides with the theatrical 4K re-release of Ken Russell's 1971 feature "The Devils" by Warner Bros. imprint Clockwork, bringing the historically controversial film back to cinemas uncut following screenings at Cannes and the London Film Festival.

## Unseen Archives and Personal Journey

Described as the definitive portrait of the elder Russell, the project follows Rupert Russell on a tragicomic exploration of his father's dual identity as both an artist and a family man. The production draws heavily upon previously unseen family archives, which include numerous personal letters, thousands of photographs, home movies, and the director's autobiographical docudrama "A British Picture."

## Star-Studded Contribution Roster

Produced in collaboration with Anna Hashmi of The Corner Shop, Andy Starke of Anti-Worlds Films, Sam Cryer of Intermission Films, and executive producer Daniel Bergmann of Stink Films, the documentary features interviews with relatives who have never previously spoken publicly. The lineup of contributors also includes prominent figures from the film and music industries, such as Guillermo del Toro, Steven Soderbergh, Oliver Stone, Kathleen Turner, Pete Townshend, Twiggy, and Ann-Margret.

Rupert Russell's prior directorial credits include the Slamdance Audience Award winner "Freedom for the Wolf," "Price Wars," and the 2025 horror release "The Last Sacrifice." The documentary's release arrives ahead of a planned retrospective of Ken Russell's cinematic works scheduled to be presented by the British Film Institute next year.
