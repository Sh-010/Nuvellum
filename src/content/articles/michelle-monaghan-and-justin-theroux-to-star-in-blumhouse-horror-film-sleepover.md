## Casting updates for horror project

Actors Michelle Monaghan and Justin Theroux are attached to star in the upcoming horror film *Sleepover*, according to an announcement from Blumhouse Atomic and Universal Pictures. Monaghan is stepping into a role previously announced to be played by Parker Posey. The feature is scheduled for release on October 8, 2027.

## Behind the camera

Director Jacob Chase, known for *Insidious: Out of the Further*, is directing the film from a screenplay he co-wrote with Bryce McGuire. The script adapts an original short story by McGuire. Jason Blum is producing the project, while executive producer roles are filled by Chase, McGuire, and Bea Sequeira. Lilly Tyson is overseeing the production for Blumhouse.

## Production background

The feature is a Cinescape Entertainment production presented by Universal Pictures and Blumhouse Atomic. The announcement coincides with Blumhouse's L.A. symposium event titled The Business of Fear.
