Two decades after its initial broadcast, the *Battlestar Galactica* season 3 episode "Exodus, Part 2" continues to stand out for its ambitious space battle sequences and narrative resolution. Written by Bradley Thompson and David Weddle and directed by Félix Enríquez Alcalá, the installment resolved the militarized occupation storyline of New Caprica.

## The Adama Maneuver

To liberate humans trapped under Cylon occupation, Admiral William Adama—played by Edward James Olmos—executed a high-risk tactical move known as the "Adama Maneuver." He jumped the titular warship, the Battlestar Galactica, directly into the atmosphere of New Caprica. 

The ship plummeted toward the settlement, deployed Viper fighter jets, and jumped back into space moments before impact. Following this sequence, Lee Adama (Jamie Bamber) arrived aboard the Pegasus to assist his father against surrounding enemy ships.

## Destroying the Pegasus

Rather than letting the Pegasus survive, the creative team decided to destroy the advanced warship to resolve production constraints, including studio space limitations and the difficulty of dividing personnel between two capital ships. Maintaining the Pegasus would have also required abandoning the series' core premise of a single battlestar protecting humanity.

Visual effects artists, who became attached to the ship, hung a "SAVE THE PEGASUS" banner outside their workspace. Ultimately, Atmosphere Visual Effects executed the climactic sequence, which featured roughly 250 to 300 elements in each compositing flow across approximately 10 shots.

In the scene, the Pegasus was set on a collision course, tearing through a Cylon basestar and sending a flaming flight pod spinning into another. The sequence earned critical recognition, winning the 2007 Emmy for Outstanding Special Visual Effects for a Series and a Visual Effects Society award, alongside nominations for direction and sound editing.
