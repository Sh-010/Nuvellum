## Creator City campus in Los Angeles

Dhar Mann Studios founder Dhar Mann spoke at MIPCOM in Cannes during the market's pre-opening headline session, outlining the vision for his upcoming studio site, Creator City. The project is a four-acre campus located in Los Angeles featuring multiple sound stages and 175 sets designed to house up to 15 productions simultaneously.

According to Mann, the facility is intended to unite creators, brands, and culture in a single location. Unlike his current studio, which uses unbranded sets, Creator City incorporates actual real-world brands as part of the production environment, such as recognizable coffee shops and retail stores. Dhar Mann Studios is collaborating with a handful of launch partners for the site, which is scheduled for its grand opening during Super Bowl week.

## Content strategy and brand partnerships

During the session, joined by Dhar Mann Studios CEO and former Disney and MTV executive Sean Atkins, Mann discussed the studio's digital-first business model. The company has expanded its relationship with Samsung to produce a new scripted series titled *Unlikely Families*, following an earlier collaboration on *Unlikely Romances*. The upcoming show is set for release in the coming months.

Dhar Mann Studios also plans to increase its output, launching one new original series on YouTube every month starting in December. Mann stated that the strategy focuses on building intellectual property through a low-cost, high-speed digital model rather than relying solely on traditional television structures, though the studio has also struck deals with media companies including Disney and Fox.

## Addressing industry shifts

Asked about the uncertainty facing television executives and staffers amid industry consolidation and fragmentation, Mann offered an encouraging perspective. He advised media professionals to focus on developing versatile skills rather than holding onto specific job titles, noting that digital-first creators are actively looking to collaborate with experienced talent from the traditional sector.
