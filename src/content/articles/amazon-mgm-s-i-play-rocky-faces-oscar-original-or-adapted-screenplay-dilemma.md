## The Category Debate for Peter Gamble’s Script

Amazon MGM Studios and the creative team behind "I Play Rocky" have engaged in discussions regarding whether Peter Gamble's screenplay will compete in the original or adapted category for the 99th Academy Awards. Because the film features preexisting characters like Rocky Balboa, Adrian, and Apollo Creed while restaging scenes from the 1976 classic, sources note that some individuals within industry organizations are weighing whether the script fits better as an adapted work.

At the same time, Gamble conceived and wrote the project on spec without basing it on a single published book, article, or memoir. Biopics and movies about the making of films have historically competed as original screenplays. However, the film was not produced under a Writers Guild of America signatory agreement, meaning the guild cannot classify the script or consider it for its own awards. This leaves the determination entirely up to the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences' Writers Branch executive committee.

## Precedent and Previous Category Shifts

The situation echoes previous awards season debates. Greta Gerwig’s "Barbie" was shifted to the adapted category after starting out with an original campaign, given that the characters originated as Mattel dolls. Other past films such as "Moonlight," "Whiplash," and "The Ballad of Buster Scruggs" also carried original labels from the WGA before the Academy moved them to adapted.

Conversely, biographical dramas and true stories built on multiple sources routinely compete as originals. Films chronicling real-life figures or behind-the-scenes Hollywood history, such as "Milk" and "Mank," have historically landed in the original category.

## Awards Circuit and Upcoming Deadlines

While the Academy deliberates, the film has already made a classification choice elsewhere on the circuit, submitting to the Golden Globes as a musical or comedy. Other ceremonies like BAFTA and the Critics Choice Awards typically follow whatever category the studio chooses to submit.

The Academy's Writers Branch executive committee reviews eligible titles and makes a final, unappealable decision. The official determination regarding "I Play Rocky" is expected to surface ahead of Oscar nominations voting opening in January 2027.
