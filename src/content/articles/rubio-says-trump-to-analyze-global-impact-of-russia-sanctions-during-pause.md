US Secretary of State Marco Rubio stated that President Donald Trump's decision to waive sweeping sanctions and tariffs on Russia will last for a 60-day period. According to Rubio, the pause is designed to analyze how the economic restrictions affect the global and US economies.

## Bipartisan criticism and global energy factors

Trump signed an executive order exempting Russia from economic restrictions that had been adopted by Congress the previous month. The law included sanctions targeting Russian officials, oligarchs, and financial institutions, while authorizing penalties on major buyers of Russian oil and gas to cut off funding for Vladimir Putin's war on Ukraine.

The executive order drew bipartisan criticism from US lawmakers. Republican Senator Thom Tillis called the move "beyond irresponsible," while Senator Ted Cruz described it as "a mistake." 

Rubio explained that various conflicts are impacting global energy prices and economies, noting the US war on Iran and attacks on Saudi Arabia by Yemen's Houthis. Trump reached a deal with Kremlin leader Vladimir Putin to allow exports of Russian diesel in exchange for the easing of sanctions, with Rubio noting that the diesel would help supply US road hauling, agriculture, and construction amid cost-of-living pressures ahead of midterm elections.

## Reactions in Ukraine

The policy shift strained relations with Kyiv. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated that he feared the United States would continue lifting restrictions on Moscow and asserted that Trump's action "rewards" Putin.
