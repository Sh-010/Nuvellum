Autonomous vehicle company Waymo has announced a partnership with automaker Toyota to supply electric vehicles for international robotaxi operations, starting with tests in Japan next year.

## Vehicle Production and Testing

Under the agreement, Toyota will manufacture 2027 bZ4X electric vehicles and integrate Waymo's self-driving technology at its Takaoka factory in Japan. Initial testing will take place in Japan ahead of a planned future robotaxi service. The deployment strategy also targets other right-hand drive nations, including the United Kingdom, where Waymo has already been testing Jaguar I-Pace vehicles in London while awaiting necessary permits.

The announcement formalizes an arrangement following an initial preliminary agreement signed 18 months prior by the two companies to explore autonomous vehicle platforms.

## Future Collaboration

Beyond commercial fleet operations, the companies indicated they will examine integrating Waymo's autonomous system into personally owned Toyota vehicles. Leadership from both firms noted that the partnership could involve future initiatives utilizing operational insights to enhance safety for road users.

Toyota executive vice president Hiroki Nakajima stated in a release that the collaboration aims to scale the technology for customers through ride-hailing services while simultaneously exploring infrastructure integrations to improve road safety.
