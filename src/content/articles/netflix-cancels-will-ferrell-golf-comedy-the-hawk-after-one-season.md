## Cancellation of 'The Hawk'

Netflix has opted to cancel the golf comedy series "The Hawk" after a single season. The program featured actor Will Ferrell in his initial regular television role since his time on "Saturday Night Live." The show initially premiered on the streaming service in July.

## Performance and Production Costs

During its release, the program achieved a spot on the platform's global top 10 list for three consecutive weeks. However, insiders indicate that production expenses, driven higher by compensation for the lead actor and co-creator, created a higher threshold for renewal that the series ultimately did not meet.

## Premise and Future Projects

The narrative centered on a former top-ranked golfer attempting a late-career comeback to secure a final major victory, despite skepticism from his family. The ensemble cast featured Molly Shannon, Jimmy Tatro, and Luke Wilson, among others. Although this particular starring vehicle concluded, the creator maintains ongoing production partnerships with the platform for multiple upcoming series.
