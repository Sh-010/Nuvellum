## Release of CJP founder

Indian police released Abhijeet Dipke, founder of the youth-led Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), from detention on Saturday. Dipke, 31, had been detained on Friday in New Delhi during a police crackdown as he headed toward a rally in the capital's central Connaught Place district.

An AFP photographer saw Dipke walk out of a police station on Saturday afternoon, waving to the media and giving a thumbs up before being driven away. CJP legal affairs chief Ratna Singh confirmed the release on social media. Student activist Neha Bora, who was also detained on Friday while trying to reach central Delhi, was released on Saturday alongside Dipke.

## Protests and demands

The demonstrations have been organized by opposition parties and CJP activists demanding the resignation of India's chief executive election commissioner, Gyanesh Kumar. Protesters accuse the election body of manipulating voter rolls to benefit Prime Minister Narendra Modi's ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP), claiming that more than 130 million voters were deleted during a recent revision. The election commission has denied any wrongdoing.

Following her release, Bora told the ANI news agency to halt the special intensive revision and impeach the chief election commissioner so ordinary citizens could have their voting rights ensured. Members of the ruling BJP have alleged that large numbers of undocumented Muslim migrants from neighboring Bangladesh have fraudulently entered the electoral rolls. Rights groups and critics have accused the Modi government of eroding democratic freedoms, allegations that the government rejects.

## Other detentions

While Dipke and Bora were freed, two other CJP leaders—Ashutosh Ranka and Saurav Das—remain in custody. They were arrested on October 14 after entering Jantar Mantar, a decades-old protest site that police have declared off-limits for demonstrations. Dipke posted on X that he was waiting for his brothers to be free, and stated on Instagram that he knew going to jail was inevitable.
