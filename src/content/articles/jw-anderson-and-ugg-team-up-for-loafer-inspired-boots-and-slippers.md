Designer Jonathan Anderson's namesake label, JW Anderson, has partnered with footwear brand Ugg for a new unisex capsule collection that reinterprets traditional silhouettes. The collaboration reimagines Ugg's Classic Boot and Tasman slipper by incorporating details inspired by penny loafers.

## Collection details and pricing

Launching on October 15, the collection includes two main styles: the JW Anderson Loafer Boot, priced at $400, and the JW Anderson Loafer, priced at $300. Both items feature rounded toes, box-stitching, and penny loafer-inspired accents while maintaining Ugg's standard cushioning and materials. The footwear is offered in chestnut, khaki, and orange shades selected by Anderson. 

The accompanying advertising campaign features musician and producer Dev Hynes, also known as Blood Orange. Styled by Anderson and photographed by Zhenya and Tanya Posternak, the campaign imagery depicts Hynes engaged in everyday routines while wearing the new designs.

## Designer and brand background

"Ugg boots are instantly recognizable, and I was interested in seeing something so familiar through the lens of JW Anderson," Anderson said in a statement regarding the partnership.

The collaboration coincides with an active period for Anderson, who was appointed creative director of Dior in 2025 following an 11-year tenure at Loewe. In addition to overseeing Dior's collections and his own label, Anderson has worked as a costume designer on films such as "Challengers" and "Queer" with director Luca Guadagnino.

For Ugg, the partnership forms part of a broader strategy of high-fashion collaborations, following recent joint collections with brands including Willy Chavarria, Telfar, Collina Strada, and Feng Chen Wang. The Ugg x JW Anderson collection will be sold through brand websites, select boutiques, and retailers such as Selfridges, The Webster, and Lane Crawford.
