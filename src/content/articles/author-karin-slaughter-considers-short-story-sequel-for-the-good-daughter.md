Following the adaptation of her 2017 novel *The Good Daughter* into a Peacock streaming series, author Karin Slaughter is exploring the possibility of continuing the story of sisters Charlie and Sam.

## Considering a Return

Slaughter, who wrote the television adaptation and acted as showrunner, shared that she has been considering a short story follow-up for the characters portrayed onscreen by Meghann Fahy and Rose Byrne. She noted that any new narrative involving the sisters would involve familiar turmoil before a resolution.

“Honestly, if they’re gonna be in a new story by Karen Slaughter, it’s never gonna be for a good thing,” she stated, outlining a trajectory where characters face difficulties before eventually recovering.

## Future Plans for Characters

The author explained that she has already mapped out potential developments for the characters, including Charlie and her partner Ben relocating to Atlanta for a fresh start.

In the original narrative, characters Charlotte and Samantha reunite decades after surviving a violent home invasion. The six-episode crime thriller series features Fahy, Byrne, and Brendan Gleeson, with direction by Steph Green.
