## The Anomaly and the Afflicted in Ellora

In the streaming series *Avatar: Seven Havens*, the Haven of Ellora faces escalating civil unrest, bandit attacks, and a growing crisis known as "The Anomaly." Expanding bright red cubes leave citizens afflicted with overwhelming fear and violent hallucinations. Among the Afflicted is Bountan, a firebender and former Bending Brigade commander who previously encountered the Anomaly spirit.

During a hospital visit, protagonists Pavi and Nisha trigger a frenzy among the Afflicted, who perceive the twin Avatars as red-eyed demons. While navigating the chaos, Nisha rapidly subdues an attacker by encasing them in stone, whereas Pavi employs earthbending to hide an frightened citizen in a cocoon, demonstrating greater restraint.

The conflict culminates when the diamond-shaped spirit monster returns. As an afflicted Bountan battles Karthik, the twins subdue Bountan using earthbending. The entity then overpowers Karthik, leading the sisters to confront it directly. Through Energy Bending, the entity passes through them and disappears, transporting the twins into a stored memory.

## Unlocking past memories

Encountering the Anomaly restores fragments of the twins' missing memories rather than afflicting them with fear. The new vision depicts them sleeping while their parents discuss a perilous journey through the Harshlands, noting they have no other choice.

This event links back to occurrences from three years prior. Karthik had previously received a message to meet a girl wearing a bracelet with a green gem, but a storm delayed him, leaving him to find only Nisha alone. Additionally, Pavi learns from a doctor that her phantom-limb pain indicates she lost the lower part of her left leg within the last few years, connecting her injury, the parents' disappearance, and Karthik finding Nisha to an incident in the Harshlands.

## Chancellor Kayoko contacts other Havens

Following the confrontation with the Anomaly spirit, episode six concludes with Chancellor Kayoko using a radio tower to broadcast a distress call to other Havens. Masked bandits known as the Faceless continue abducting benders, and growing numbers of citizens become Afflicted, leaving Ellora short of protectors. Urged by Jae after a small riot by scared benders, Kayoko’s broadcast breaks the isolation that has defined the series thus far as the city calls for aid.
