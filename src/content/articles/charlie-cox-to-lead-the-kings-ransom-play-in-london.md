Actor Charlie Cox is returning to the London stage this winter to star in Stuart Slade’s new play, “The King’s Ransom.” The production will hold its world premiere at the Donmar Warehouse.

## Plot and character details

Set in 1977 Memphis, the play follows a small-time crook named Vince Black, played by Cox, who is presented with an unusual proposition. According to the production's synopsis, the plan involves kidnapping Elvis Presley's corpse, storing it in a chest freezer, and collecting a million-dollar ransom. Vince recruits a team of misfits and dropouts to carry out the heist.

## Cast and creative team

The ensemble cast features Blake Harrison as Rusty, Tom Fletcher as Charlie, Matthew Flynn as Mr. Bluefield, Leo Patrick as Jessie Lee, Jenny Rainsford as Rhonda/Jenny, Christopher Short as Elvis, Cherrelle Skeete as Mike, and Ronnie Yorke as Red. The role of Martha is shared by Ruby Barker, Eira Kay, and Trixie Willis.

Matthew Dunster directs the production. The creative team includes set designer Anna Fleischle, costume designer Jean Chan, lighting designer Sally Ferguson, sound designer Holly Khan, illusion designer Chris Cox, fight and intimacy directors Ruth Cooper-Brown and Rachel Bown-Williams of Rc-Annie, and casting director Lotte Hines CDG.

## Run dates and theater history

“The King’s Ransom” is scheduled to open on Dec. 16, following previews beginning Dec. 5, and will run through Feb. 6, 2027.

Cox previously performed at the Donmar Warehouse in a 2010 production of “The Prince of Homburg.” His other theatrical credits include the 2019 West End revival of Harold Pinter’s “Betrayal,” which transferred to Broadway.
