## Developing new projects at Absurd Ventures

Dan Houser, co-founder and former head writer of Rockstar Games, has shared initial details regarding two video game titles currently in development at his media company, Absurd Ventures. Houser launched the studio in 2023 following his departure from Rockstar in 2020.

Speaking on an episode of Variety's podcast, Houser stated that one of the upcoming titles is an open-world comedy focused on character memory and comedy, with more information expected in the following months. The second project is a dystopian science-fiction game centered on a video game development project that went wrong, featuring a rogue artificial intelligence that achieved sentience but dislikes itself because it was created by two people who did not get along.

## Building transmedia universes

The untitled games form part of a broader transmedia strategy led by Houser and longtime writing partner Jeffrey “Lazlow” Jones. Absurd Ventures is developing original intellectual properties across three main worlds: “A Better Paradise,” “American Caper,” and the “Absurdaverse.” The studio is rolling out graphic novels, comics, fiction podcasts, books, and animated series alongside the planned video games.

## Creative isolation and avoiding comparison

To maintain a distinct creative direction, Houser avoids consuming media that could influence or distract him. He stated that he does not play open-world games anymore because he finds himself deconstructing their design and technical strengths and weaknesses in real time. 

Similarly, Houser avoided watching external media while working on past projects, noting that he did not watch television shows like “The Sopranos” or consume other Westerns while working on "Red Dead Redemption" to prevent creative disruption. He also avoids promotional material for upcoming titles in the industry, such as extended trailers for "Grand Theft Auto VI."
