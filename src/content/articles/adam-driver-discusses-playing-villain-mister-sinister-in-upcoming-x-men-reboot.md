Actor Adam Driver discussed his upcoming role as the villain Mister Sinister in Marvel's upcoming "X-Men" reboot during an appearance on "Late Night with Seth Meyers" on Monday.

## Joining the Marvel Franchise

Driver is set to play Dr. Nathaniel Essex, also known as Mister Sinister, in the Jake Schreier-directed film. During the interview, host Seth Meyers displayed a photograph of the character in full costume, prompting the actor to joke about the early morning appearance of the look. When asked about his prior familiarity with the character, Driver noted he was previously more aware of Magneto, recalling childhood memories of enjoying the franchise with his cousin.

Driver also mentioned that his 10-year-old son is enthusiastic about the project, marking a change from previous films in his career that his child had little interest in viewing. Marvel head Kevin Feige previously noted that the studio had pursued Driver for various roles over the years prior to his casting in the franchise.

## Cast and Release Date

Directed by Schreier, the upcoming "X-Men" reboot features an ensemble cast including Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Inde Navarrette as Rogue, Kit Connor as Cyclops, Maya Boyd as Storm, Asa Germann as Angel, and Christopher Abbott as Charles Xavier. The film is scheduled to release in theaters on May 5, 2028.
