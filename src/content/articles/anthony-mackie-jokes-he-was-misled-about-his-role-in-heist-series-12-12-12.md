Actor Anthony Mackie revealed that his experience starring in the Apple TV heist thriller *12 12 12* differed significantly from how the project was initially presented to him. 

## Pitch versus reality

In the series, Mackie portrays FBI Special Agent Jack Strickland, who pursues criminal mastermind Paul Galvin, played by Jamie Dornan, and his brother Bobby, played by Jack Kesy. Mackie stated that the project was pitched to him as a new version of *The Italian Job*. He noted with a laugh that the comparison was inaccurate, adding that he would not have taken the role otherwise because he missed out on driving a Mini Cooper.

Dornan also faced a unique approach from series creators Dudi Appleton and Jim Keeble during his casting process. The actor admitted he did not watch a French film they sent him for homework, preferring influences like *Ocean's Twelve* and *Ocean's Thirteen* instead.

## A complex production structure

*12 12 12* takes its name from FBI crime principles, which dictate that high-value robberies take 12 months to plan, 12 hours to execute, and leave a 12-day window for law enforcement to catch the culprits. The series splits episodes across three different timelines corresponding to these periods. 

Dornan described the filming process as difficult and headache-inducing due to the scattered production schedule. Because the narrative jumps across timelines and locations, actors frequently worked on scenes from different blocks with multiple directors on the same day, requiring careful tracking of character developments.

The series premiered on November 13, with new episodes releasing weekly on Fridays through December 25.
