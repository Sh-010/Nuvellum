## Broadway Return After Fourteen Years

Tom Hanks and Kelli O’Hara are scheduled to star in a Broadway production of *This World of Tomorrow* during the fall of 2027. The limited engagement will be directed by Tony Award winner Kenny Leon at an unannounced Shubert theater. Producer Brian Anthony Moreland announced the upcoming run in association with The Shed, featuring artistic director Alex Poots and chief executive officer Meredith Hodges.

The upcoming production marks Hanks' return to Broadway for the first time in over fourteen years, following his Tony-nominated role in Nora Ephron's *Lucky Guy*. The play itself is adapted from a collection of short stories written by Hanks and originally premiered at The Shed in an eight-week sold-out off-broadway run.

## Plot and Creative Team

The narrative follows a character named Bert Allenberry at the end of the twenty-first century who repeatedly travels back to a specific day at the 1939 New York World's Fair in Queens. The storyline explores themes of time travel, technology, and the choices individuals make for love.

Director Kenny Leon expressed enthusiasm for continuing the project with the two lead performers. Additional creative team members and casting details will be shared at a later date.
