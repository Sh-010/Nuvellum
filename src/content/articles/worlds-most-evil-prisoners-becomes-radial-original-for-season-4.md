## Season 4 expansion and distribution

The British-produced crime series *World’s Most Evil Prisoners* has been picked up as a Radial Entertainment original for its upcoming fourth season. The ten-part run is produced by UK-based Woodcut Media and is scheduled to premiere in 2027, following the start of production in September.

Under the distribution arrangement, the new season will be available exclusively on Radial in the United States, Canada, Germany, Austria, and Switzerland. Woodcut International is managing sales for all other territories, introducing the title to buyers at the MIPCOM Cannes market. The first three seasons of the series are already available on Radial's free-streaming services as part of the broader *World’s Most Evil* franchise.

## Focus of the franchise

The series profiles offenders serving life sentences who commit further offenses—such as torture, kidnapping, and murder—while incarcerated. Episodes incorporate accounts from victims, friends, family members, journalists, and police officers.

"Prisoners examines how offenders manipulate and harm people behind bars, where confinement changes their methods and who is vulnerable," said Cristina Guggino, VP of Originals & Content Partnerships at Radial. Koulla Anastasi, Managing Director of Woodcut International, added that the fourth season explores the psychology behind the crimes while aiming to maintain sensitivity toward victims' families.

## Production background

Negotiations for the fourth season deal were conducted by Katie Carroll, Director of Originals & Business Development for Radial Entertainment, and Koulla Anastasi for Woodcut International. Radial Entertainment is backed by funds managed by Oaktree Capital Management and maintains a library exceeding 75,000 titles spanning true crime, reality, animation, action, and westerns. Woodcut Media's catalog also includes programs such as *World’s Most Evil Killers*, *Britain’s Most Evil Killers*, *Confessions of a Female Serial Killer*, and *The Murder of Meredith*.
