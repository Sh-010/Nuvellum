## The audiobook speed debate

For fans of the LitRPG series *Dungeon Crawler Carl*, listening speeds for the popular audiobooks performed by Jeff Hays have long been a subject of discussion across online communities like Reddit, Facebook, and TikTok. While some listeners report playing the audio at double speed or faster, author Matt Dinniman addressed the practice during an interview at New York Comic Con.

Dinniman stated that he personally listens to most audiobooks between 1.0 and 1.2 speed, occasionally reaching 1.25. While acknowledging that listeners still absorb the story at higher speeds, he noted that settings above 1.3 begin to affect the performance delivery.

"Jeff Hays' delivery is really good, and there's nothing wrong with listening to them at 1.2, but I see people doing it at 2.3 and it's like chipmunks on bath salts," Dinniman said. He added that accelerated speeds cause listeners to miss out on the nuance of Hays' performance.

## Future projects and adaptations

Aside from addressing audiobook speeds, Dinniman discussed the status of ongoing and future projects within the *Dungeon Crawler Carl* universe. He confirmed he is currently working on the final installment of the series, noting that the project is set to be split into two books.

Additionally, a live-action television series based on the books is currently in development. Dinniman mentioned that the adaptation is being handled by fans who are protective of the source material, though scripts are not yet finished and major adaptation changes remain to be seen.

A graphic novel side story centered on the character Florin, titled *Crocodile*, is also scheduled for release following a BackerKit campaign. Written as a short story treatment by Dinniman and scripted by Michael Moreci with art by Brett Bean, the project could pave the way for additional character spinoffs if reception proves favorable.
