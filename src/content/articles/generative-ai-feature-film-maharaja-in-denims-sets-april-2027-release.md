Intelliflicks Studios is preparing to release its feature-length generative AI film, *Maharaja in Denims*, by April 2027 to coincide with the Punjabi harvest festival Baisakhi. Based on Khushwant Singh’s novel, the narrative follows a young man experiencing flashbacks to three distinct past lives, including 19th-century Sikh ruler Maharaja Ranjit Singh, alongside victims of the 1947 Partition and the 1984 anti-Sikh violence. 

## Production and technological evolution

An initial screen adaptation was optioned in July 2020 by producer Guneet Monga Kapoor, but the project stalled due to pandemic disruptions and projected high costs. The AI approach materialized after Gurdeep Pall, formerly of Microsoft, suggested utilizing generative tools during a 2023 visit to Chandigarh. Pall and Singh subsequently co-founded Intelliflicks to test the viability of creating a realistic feature.

The production relied on a shifting array of platforms, transitioning through Midjourney, Runway, Google Veo, Nano Banana, Kling AI, Seedance 2.0, and Higgsfield. Maintaining visual continuity across changing camera angles proved challenging, requiring the team to rebuild portions of the film multiple times as software capabilities improved. Despite the technological scope, the production budget exceeded $500,000, excluding marketing expenses.

## Human oversight and musical score

While software assisted with visual generation, studio executives emphasized that the creative direction remained entirely human-led. Screenwriting, storyboarding, and final artistic decisions stayed with the core filmmaking crew, directed by Punjab-based independent filmmaker Balpreet Kaur, who holds final creative control.

In contrast to the visual generation, the audio elements are entirely human-created. The project features studio-recorded dialogue, professional narration, and original songs composed and performed by human artists. Ahmedabad-based Twenty-Twenty One Studios has joined the project as a co-producer, evaluating options for a theatrical release followed by non-theatrical distribution.
