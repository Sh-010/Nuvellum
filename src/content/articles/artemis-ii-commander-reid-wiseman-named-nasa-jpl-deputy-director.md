Former astronaut and Artemis II Commander Reid Wiseman has been appointed as Caltech associate vice president and deputy director of NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory. The retired U.S. Navy captain is scheduled to begin his duties on November 30, 2026, at the Southern California laboratory managed by Caltech for NASA.

## Background and Career

Wiseman joined NASA’s astronaut corps in 2009. His career includes serving as a flight engineer aboard the International Space Station during a 2014 mission and commanding an underwater habitat expedition in 2016. From December 2020 through November 2022, he managed NASA's astronaut corps as chief astronaut, overseeing flight readiness and crew safety. 

As commander of the Artemis II test flight alongside Victor Glover, Christina Koch, and Jeremy Hansen, Wiseman traveled further from Earth than any previous human during a lunar flyby spanning nearly 700,000 miles.

## New Leadership Role

In his upcoming position, Wiseman will oversee strategic leadership, operations management, and institutional planning at the laboratory. He will also act as the primary interface for national security programs and serve as Center Management Authority for mission execution.

NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman praised the appointment, stating that Wiseman possesses the technical judgment and operational experience needed for the position. Caltech President Ray Jayawardhana and JPL Director Dave Gallagher also welcomed the incoming deputy director, highlighting his exploration background and leadership capabilities as the institution approaches its 90th anniversary.
