## European Interior Ministers Discuss Migration Policy in Munich

European interior ministers are gathering in Munich for talks focused on tightening the bloc's migration policy, strengthening external borders, and accelerating deportations. A spokesperson for German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt stated that the discussions will address the implementation of a policy shift and the further refinement of the European Migration Pact.

Seventeen ministers are participating in the meeting alongside EU Commissioner for Internal Affairs and Migration Magnus Brunner. In addition, Germany, Denmark, Austria, the Netherlands, and Greece plan to hold separate discussions regarding the potential creation of deportation centers or return hubs in third countries, with Germany aiming to secure an agreement with a nation in Africa or Asia by the end of the year. Proposals also include transforming the EU border agency, Frontex, into a border protection and return agency.

## Munich and Cologne Vie for German Olympic Bid

Germany's Olympic Sports Confederation (DOSB) is set to decide whether Munich or Cologne-Rhine-Ruhr will serve as the candidate city for a future Olympic Games bid, targeting the 2036, 2040, or 2044 events. The decision follows Berlin's withdrawal from the process earlier in the week.

Germany has kept its options open regarding which year to target. The 2036 Games are widely anticipated to be hosted by an Asian country, and the year is politically sensitive as it marks the centenary of the 1936 Berlin Olympics held during the Nazi era. Alternatively, some policymakers advocate for a 2040 bid to coincide with the 50th anniversary of German reunification. 

Munich intends to host the majority of events within city limits, utilizing existing infrastructure from the 1972 Games such as the Olympic Park. Meanwhile, the Cologne-Rhine-Ruhr proposal distributes events across multiple locations, including swimming in Schalke and basketball and football matches in Düsseldorf and at Borussia Dortmund's stadium.
