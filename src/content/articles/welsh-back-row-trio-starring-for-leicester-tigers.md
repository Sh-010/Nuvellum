Having never played together previously, the trio has started all three of Leicester's league fixtures, securing victories against Newcastle and Gloucester alongside a narrow defeat to Saracens.

Wainwright and Reffell have operated as flankers, while Cracknell anchors the scrum at number eight. Reffell described his teammates as physical and dynamic players whose game intelligence ensures they are positioned effectively on the field.

## Contrasting journeys to Leicester

The three players arrived at Welford Road at different stages of their careers. Wainwright joined Leicester following nearly a decade at Dragons, adapting quickly to his new surroundings with the help of a lift-share arrangement with Reffell and fly-half Billy Searle. His early performances earned praise from team-mate Gabriel Hamer-Webb, who noted his high work rate and passing ability.
