## A gothic fairy tale on streaming

Tim Burton's 2005 stop-motion film *Corpse Bride* is available to stream on HBO Max. Directed by Tim Burton and Mike Johnson, the 77-minute movie follows Victor Van Dort (voiced by Johnny Depp), whose arranged marriage to Victoria Everglot (Emily Watson) is disrupted when he accidentally proposes to an undead bride named Emily (Helena Bonham Carter) in the woods.

The film's narrative draws inspiration from "The Finger," a Jewish folktale collected in *Lilith's Cave: Jewish Tales of the Supernatural* by author Howard Schwartz. Co-screenwriter John August noted that the production adapted the story to fit a family-friendly, universal audience, giving the undead bride a more peaceful conclusion and modifying the characters.

## Directorial collaboration and visual style

While Burton had previously produced stop-motion films such as *The Nightmare Before Christmas* and *James and the Giant Peach*, *Corpse Bride* marked his first stop-motion directing credit. Because he was simultaneously occupied with *Charlie and the Chocolate Factory*, Burton took a backseat as co-director, leaving Mike Johnson to manage daily operations with the crew while Burton maintained exclusive control over storyboards, concepts, and scene approvals.

The production is also notable as the first official project for independent stop-motion studio Laika, which co-produced the film after current CEO Travis Knight took over Will Vinton Studios. The movie establishes a stark visual contrast between the dreary, gray world of the living and the vibrant, colorful land of the dead.
