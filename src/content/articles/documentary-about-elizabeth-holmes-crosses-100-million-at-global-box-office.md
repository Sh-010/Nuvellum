## Box office milestone achieved

The documentary film "You Can See Everything," focusing on Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes, has surpassed $100 million globally at the box office. Created by filmmakers Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim and distributed by independent studio A24, the feature reached the milestone over the weekend, according to industry reporting.

## Domestic dominance and budget performance

Variety reported that the vast majority of the film's $102.4 million total stems from domestic ticket sales, accounting for $93.4 million. The commercial performance represents a notable return for A24, particularly given the production's reported budget of $7 million.

## Upcoming international rollout

The project is slated to expand into additional international territories in the coming months. Screenings are scheduled to begin in Mexico, Brazil, and Argentina on November 25 and 26, which could push the overall earnings higher as the release widens.
