## Stock surge follows acquisition report

Shares of Lionsgate experienced a sharp increase, rising more than 15 percent during afternoon trading. The upward movement followed media reports that Legendary Entertainment submitted an offer to acquire the standalone studio. According to Bloomberg, discussions between the entities have restarted, with Legendary presenting a formal bid.

Legendary, backed by management and private equity firm Apollo, has previously explored a potential transaction. Media reports initially highlighted Legendary's interest in June 2025, following the separation of Lionsgate Studios from Starz. Representatives for Lionsgate declined to provide a statement regarding the reports.

## Potential industry impact

An agreement between the two companies would provide Legendary with its own theatrical distribution division. Currently, Legendary distributes films through Skydance's Paramount. Additionally, the deal would incorporate Lionsgate's large film and television library, which generates substantial cash flow.

Lionsgate has frequently been viewed as an acquisition target amid ongoing media industry consolidation. Driven by speculation surrounding the potential buyout, the company's stock climbed past $12, marking roughly double its 52-week high.
