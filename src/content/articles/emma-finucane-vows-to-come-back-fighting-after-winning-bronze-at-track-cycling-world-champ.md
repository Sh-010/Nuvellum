## Narrow defeat in Shanghai

Emma Finucane stated she will "come back fighting" for gold medals following a narrow miss at the Track Cycling World Championships in Shanghai. The 23-year-old took bronze in the keirin event on Sunday after leading into the home straight.

Finucane finished 14 thousandths of a second behind neutral athlete Alina Lysenko, who took the top spot, and Germany's Lea Friedrich. The bronze added to silver medals she won earlier in the week in both the team sprint and the individual sprint, with Chinese cyclists claiming gold in both of those events.

## Building towards the Los Angeles Olympics

Reflecting on her performance across the championships, Finucane noted that she had never previously medalled in all three races at a world championship event. Her previous achievements include winning a gold medal in the women's team sprint alongside Katy Marchant and Sophie Capewell at the Paris Olympics in 2024, alongside individual bronze medals in the sprint and keirin.

In Shanghai, Rhianna Parris-Smith stepped into the team sprint alongside Finucane following Marchant's retirement, with the British trio finishing behind the Chinese team of Jiang Yulu, Tong Mengqi, and Yuan Liying.

Finucane pointed to future goals, identifying the 2028 Olympic Games in Los Angeles as a major target. She emphasized that internal competition within the British squad would help drive progress, noting that numerous athletes are contending for team selection.

Great Britain finished at the top of the overall medal table at the championships, securing 12 medals, which included three gold medals won by Anna Morris.
