## Flight grounding and security response

Air travel across the United States came to a standstill on a Friday as a rare nationwide ground stop halted more than 10,000 flights. Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin addressed the public following the suspension, clarifying that the disruption stemmed from a telecommunication outage rather than a security attack.

While the transportation sector faced widespread disruptions, President Donald Trump did not immediately issue a public statement concerning the aviation halt. Instead, his public focus remained on economic and legislative matters involving the entertainment industry.

## Push for the film incentive bill

Trump turned to social media to promote the Motion Picture, Television, and Entertainment Revitalization Act. He stated that bipartisan meetings were being organized to advance the legislation, naming numerous senators and representatives from both political parties involved in the effort.

According to the administration's messaging, the proposed measure aims to bring entertainment production back to the United States. The proposal argues that tax incentives offered abroad have drawn production away, and that supporting domestic creators would aid blue-collar workers and benefit federal revenues.
