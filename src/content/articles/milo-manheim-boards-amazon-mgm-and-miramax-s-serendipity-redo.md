Actor Milo Manheim has joined the cast of the upcoming romantic comedy redo of *Serendipity*, directed by Michael Showalter for Amazon MGM Studios and Miramax. Specific details regarding the role Manheim will portray have not yet been disclosed.

## A contemporary 'rebuttal'

Announced in August, Showalter's production is positioned as a contemporary "rebuttal" to the original 2001 film rather than a direct remake or reboot, focusing instead on a new set of characters. Marc Klein, who wrote the original film, penned the script for the new project.

Production duties are being handled by Showalter and Jordana Mollick through Semi-Formal Productions, alongside Simon Fields, who also produced the 2001 release. Executive producers for Miramax include Jonathan Glickman, Alexandra Loewy, and Thom Zadra, alongside Klein.

## Growing film and television slate

Manheim's recent credits include appearances in the Paramount+ series *School Spirits* and Eli Roth's horror film *Thanksgiving*, in addition to his work in the Disney Channel *Zombies* franchise. He is also slated to star as Flynn Rider in Disney's upcoming live-action adaptation of *Tangled*, scheduled for a theatrical release on March 31, 2028.

It remains unconfirmed whether the new *Serendipity* project is intended for theatrical release or streaming.
