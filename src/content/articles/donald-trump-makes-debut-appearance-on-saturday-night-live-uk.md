## Trump's debut on SNL UK

Donald Trump made his first-ever appearance on Sky’s *Saturday Night Live UK*, portrayed by recurring actor James Austin Johnson during the show's cold open.

Johnson's character appeared alongside Andy Burnham, played by George Fouracres, and Volodymyr Zelenskyy, played by Freddie Meredith. During the sketch, Burnham discussed issues facing the Ukrainian leader before the entrance of Trump, who remarked on his sudden appearance by referencing Beetlejuice.

## Sketch topics and production details

Trump offered Burnham a job swap during the skit, describing the U.S. presidency as an "awful job" in a "terrible place full of disgusting people." The dialogue also touched on various UK news stories, including Manchester City, the small boats crisis, a tiger mauling incident, and comedian Russell Brand, who is currently on trial at the High Court.

The episode marked the fifth installment of the first half of Season 2, hosted by James Norton with musical guest The Streets. *Saturday Night Live UK* is produced by Universal Television Alternative Studio’s UK production team alongside Broadway Video, with Lorne Michaels serving as executive producer and James Longman as lead producer.
