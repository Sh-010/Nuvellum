The 1984 sci-fi action thriller *The Terminator* is currently available to stream for free with ads on YouTube.

## Plot and premise

Directed and co-written by James Cameron, the film follows two time travelers from the year 2029 who arrive in Los Angeles in 1984. A cyborg assassin known as a Terminator, played by Arnold Schwarzenegger, is sent back in time to murder Sarah Connor, portrayed by Linda Hamilton. In the future, machines control the world, and Sarah's unborn son, John Connor, is destined to lead the human resistance. 

To counter the threat, the human resistance sends a trusted soldier, Kyle Reese, played by Michael Biehn, back to 1984 to protect Sarah from the Terminator.

## Influences and legacy

In interviews, Cameron has cited John Carpenter and his film *Halloween* as key influences on the project, viewing *The Terminator* as a low-budget slasher movie. Featuring a slower, moodier pace than its 1991 sequel *Terminator 2: Judgment Day*, the original film established Schwarzenegger as a global superstar through his stoic, unfeeling performance as the robotic antagonist.

Upon its release, *The Terminator* was a major commercial success and helped expand audience expectations for serious-minded, high-concept adult science fiction.
