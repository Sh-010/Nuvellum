Nearly 40 years after the release of the original *Hellraiser* movie, creator Clive Barker has indicated that the video game *Hellraiser: Revival* has sparked numerous concepts for the future of the film franchise. 

## Video game release inspires new concepts

Following the release of Saber Interactive’s *Hellraiser: Revival* video game, Barker stated in an interview with *Dread Central* that what was achieved in the game served as a "wake-up call" for anyone involved in the franchise. He noted that the project encouraged revisiting existing worlds in novel ways, adding that he intends to remain involved in the universe's development.

“I have many ideas that I’d like to see realized in the Hellraiser universe, and I’m planning to keep my fingers in that pie,” Barker said.

## Franchise history and background

Based on his 1986 novella *The Hellbound Heart*, Barker wrote and directed the original 1987 horror film, which introduced Pinhead—played by Doug Bradley—and the Cenobites. The property subsequently expanded into 11 films, alongside books, comics, and video games.

Barker successfully reclaimed United States rights to the franchise in 2020 and later served as a producer on the 2022 Hulu reboot directed by David Bruckner.
