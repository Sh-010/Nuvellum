Unconfirmed online leaks on Reddit and Discord suggest that Wizards of the Coast is preparing a new collaboration for *Magic: The Gathering* based on Frank Herbert's science fiction franchise. The alleged superdrop, reportedly titled "My Arrakis, My Secret Lair," includes three distinct drops featuring new characters, valuable reprints, and thematic designs.

## New legendary creatures

The first leaked drop focuses on iconic figures from the universe, introducing eight new legendary cards for Commander players. Notable cards include Paul Atreides in two variants—a white creature utilizing the Training mechanic named Paul Atreides, Ducal Heir, and a self-mill synergy card representing his ascension titled Muad'Dib, Prophet Foretold. Other reported additions feature Padishah Emperor Shaddam IV and Chani, Blade of Sietch Tabr, which is designed to synergize with desert cards.

## Reprints and sandworm mechanics

A second leaked drop centers on valuable staples, containing five reprints with an estimated average value of $75. This selection reportedly includes high-cost artifact and treasure cards such as Darksteel Forge and Revel in Riches, alongside Halo Fountain, Hedge Shredder, and Cultivator Caravan. A third drop introduces a unique sandworm card named Shai-Hulud, featuring Trample, Indestructible, and an ability to destroy artifacts and enchantments while fetching basic lands or deserts.

While the leaks remain unconfirmed by the publisher, community reaction online has been generally positive regarding the thematic flavor and artwork compared to other recent crossover properties.
